# The Bahamas — Statute Law of The Bahamas (LawCodex)

Current consolidated Acts of **The Commonwealth of The Bahamas** as plain Markdown, converted from the
official PDFs of **Bahamas Legislation** (Office of the Attorney General — Law Revision Centre) at
<https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs>.

Retrieved: 2026-10-13 (point-in-time "current legislation" view as of that date).

## Contents

| Directory | What | Files |
|---|---|---|
| `constitution/` | The Constitution of The Commonwealth of The Bahamas (legislation no. 1973-1080) | 1 |
| `chapters/` | Acts forming part of the Statute Law revised edition, with a chapter number (`ch-NNN-<slug>.md`) | 374 |
| `acts/` | Other current Acts without a chapter number in the PDF — local/private Acts, appropriation Acts and recent Acts not yet assigned a chapter (`<legislation-number>-<slug>.md`) | 237 |

Total: **612 of the 680 principal Acts** listed under *Acts only → by alphabetical order* for the current
date. Front matter: `title`, `identifier` (Ch. number, when printed), `legislation_number`, `type`,
`date` (commencement as listed), `long_title`, `category`, `source`, `source_pdf`, `pages`, `retrieved`.

Marginal notes (side headings and amendment references) are rendered as *italic* lines before the
paragraph they sit beside; section numbers are in **bold**. Running headers/footers
("STATUTE LAW OF THE BAHAMAS", "[Original Service …]", "LRO …") are removed.

## Not covered

- Subsidiary legislation (regulations, rules, orders) — available on the site but not included.
- Amending Acts as separate documents (their effect is in the consolidated principal Acts), Bills, and
  repealed / not-in-force legislation.
- Tables/forms are flattened to text; check the PDF for complex tables.

## Regenerate

```
python3 scripts/bs_fetch.py     # posts the A–Z point-in-time form, downloads principal Act PDFs -> ../_raw/bs/pdf
python3 scripts/bs_convert.py   # pdftotext -bbox-layout -> Markdown (scripts/bs_pdfmd.py)
```

Requires poppler (`pdftotext`, `pdfinfo`) and `scripts/requirements.txt`. Downloads are streamed with
HTTP Range resume and verified (header, `%%EOF`, `pdfinfo`, size) before conversion.
