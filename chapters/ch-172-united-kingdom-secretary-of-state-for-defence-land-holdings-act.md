### UNITED KINGDOM SECRETARY OF STATE FOR DEFENCE (LAND HOLDINGS)

*10 of 1992*

An Act to confer upon Her Majesty’s Secretary of State for Defence in the United Kingdom authority to hold and dispose of certain lands situate in The Bahamas.

[Assent 4th May, 1992]

[Commencement 1st April, 1964]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the United Kingdom Secretary of State for Defence (Land Holdings) Act, 1992.

*Section 2(2) United Kingdom Act, Ch. 15 of 1964, to have effect in The Bahamas.*

**2.** As from the date of the coming into operation of this Act, section 2(2) of the United Kingdom Defence (Transfer of Functions) Act, 1964 shall be deemed to have had effect in The Bahamas and the authority conferred by that section upon the Secretary of State for Defence shall be deemed to include the power to hold and dispose of land or any interest in land in The Bahamas.
