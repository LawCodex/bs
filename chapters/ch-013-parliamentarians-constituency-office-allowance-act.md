### PARLIAMENTARIANS (CONSTITUENCY OFFICE ALLOWANCE)

*30 of 1998 32 of 2015*

An Act to make provision for the payment of an allowance to members of Parliament for the establishment and maintenance of a Constituency Office and for purposes connected thereto.

[Assent 6th August, 1998] [Commencement 1st October, 1997] *

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Parliamentarians (Constituency Office Allowance) Act.

*Payment of allowance.*

*First Schedule.*

*Second Schedule.*

**2.** (1) There shall be paid to every Member of Parliament elected to a constituency in New Providence, Grand Bahama and Bimini an annual allowance specified in Part I of the First Schedule and such allowance shall be used for the establishment and maintenance of a constituency office and for purposes specified in Part I of the Second Schedule.

*First Schedule.*

*Second Schedule.*

(2) There shall be paid to every Member of Parliament elected to a constituency in a Family Island (other than Grand Bahama and Bimini) an annual allowance specified in Part II of the First Schedule and such an allowance shall be used for the establishment and maintenance of a constituency office, for purposes specified in Part II of the Second Schedule and for carrying out duties in relation to the constituency.

(3) No Member of Parliament shall use a constituency office or allow a constituency office to be used for any public political meeting or fund raising activity for any political party.

*Accounts and audit.*

**3.** (1) Every Member of Parliament shall keep proper accounts and retain receipts of payments made for the purposes of this Act and such accounts shall be audited annually by the Auditor-General.
