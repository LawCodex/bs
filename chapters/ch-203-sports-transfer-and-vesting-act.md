# Sports Transfer and Vesting Act

### SPORTS TRANSFER AND VESTING

*11 of 2000*

SPORTS TRANSFER AND VESTING An Act to transfer and vest property in the Minister responsible for Housing and the Treasurer of the Commonwealth of The Bahamas.

[Assent 13th March, 2000]

[Commencement 1st January, 1997]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Sports Transfer and Vesting Act, 2000.

*Vesting in Minister responsible for Housing. First Schedule. Ch. 385. Vesting in Treasurer. Second Schedule. Ch. 385.*

**2.** All property referred to in the First Schedule vested in the Minister responsible for Sports under the Sports Act is transferred to and vested in the Minister responsible for Housing in fee simple.

**3.** All property referred to in the Second Schedule vested in the Minister responsible for Sports under the Sports Act is transferred to and vested in the Treasurer of the Commonwealth of The Bahamas in fee simple.
