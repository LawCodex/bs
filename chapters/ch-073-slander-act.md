# Slander Act

### SLANDER

## ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS SECTION

*24 of 1909*

SLANDER An Act relating to the law of slander.

[Commencement 23rd August, 1909]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Slander Act.

*Slander actionable without proof of special damage in certain cases.*

**2.** Words spoken and published of any person imputing adultery, unchastity, fornication, incest, incontinence or drunkenness shall be actionable without proof of special damage:

Provided that in any such action the plaintiff shall not receive more costs than damages, unless the court shall certify that there was reasonable ground for bringing the action.
