## PART II VESTING OF PROPERTY IN BTC

*Vesting of Property.*

*First Schedule.*

**4.** (1) There is vested in BTC and or confirmed as vesting in BTC all those freehold properties listed in the First Schedule, in respect of which title documents have not been issued or have been lost, which are or were previously Crown lands coming under the use, management, control, operation, possession and or ownership of BaTelCo pursuant or subsequent to the Corporation Act and became, or should have become, vested in BTC pursuant to the Order.

*Second Schedule.*

(2) The Treasurer shall, contemporaneously with the privatisation of BTC, execute in favour of BTC a conveyance of all those freehold properties currently vested in the Treasurer and listed in the Second Schedule which were vested in the Treasurer for purposes of telecommunications by virtue of —

*12 of 1964.*

(a) the Public Boards (Divesting of Title) Act; and

*Ch. 252.*

(b) the Acquisition of Land Act.

*Third Schedule.*

(3) All conveyances by the Treasurer to BaTelCo of the freehold properties listed in the Third Schedule and any subsequent conveyances of such properties by BaTelCo to BTC made before the date on which this Act comes into force are hereby confirmed and validated.

## PART III REAL PROPERTY TAX

*Real Property Tax.*

*Ch. 375.*

**5.** For the period commencing from the date of the Order up to (and including) the Privatisation Date there shall be exempt from real property taxes under the Real Property Tax Act all properties owned by BTC and or vested, or to be vested, in BTC pursuant to this Act.
