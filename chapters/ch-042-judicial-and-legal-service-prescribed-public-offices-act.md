### CHAPTER 42 JUDICIAL AND LEGAL SERVICE (PRESCRIBED PUBLIC OFFICES)

An Act to prescribe the public offices to which Article 117 of The Constitution applies.

[Commencement 30th December, 1974]

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Judicial and Legal Service (Prescribed Public Offices) Act.

*Prescribed public offices.*

*Schedule.*

**2.** The public offices set forth in the first column of the Schedule, being offices in the Department set forth in the second column thereof, are hereby prescribed as the public offices to which Article 117 of The Constitution applies.

*Power of Governor-General to amend Schedule.*

**3.** The Governor-General may by order amend the Schedule by adding thereto or deleting therefrom any public office.
