### SKIING AND MOTOR BOAT CONTROL ACT

Preliminary

*Short*

**1.** title.

This Act may be cited as the Water Skiing and Motor Boat Control Act.

**2.** Interpretation.

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires —

1 “authorised officer" means a member of the Royal Bahamas Police Force, a member of the Royal Bahamas Defence Force, an officer of the Port Department or any port authority established by law, any officer of a Local Government Council acting within the scope of his authority under the Local Government Act, or any public officer designated in writing by the Minister or the Authority;

2 "Authority" means the Port Controller or a port authority in a port area as defined under the Port Authorities Act;

"hours of darkness” means the period extending from one hour after sunset until one hour before sunrise;

3 "fixed penalty notice" means a notice given under section 8B; 4 "jet ski" means a motor boat designed to be operated by one or more persons sitting, standing, or kneeling on the vessel and includes any similar vessel propelled by a jet pump or similar device;

“Minister” means the Minister responsible for Maritime Affairs; 5 “motor boat” means every description of vessel being propelled by mechanical power, either for recreational use or licensed under the Fisheries Act to engage in commercial fishing, but does not include a sailing vessel fitted with an auxiliary engine rated at less than ten horse power;

6 "register" means the register of motor boats maintained by the Port Controller or Family Island Administrator in accordance with section 10;

7 “registered motor boat" means a motor boat registered in accordance with this Act;

“restricted zone” means that area of water lying within two hundred feet of the shoreline in any part of The Bahamas to which for the time being section 3 applies;

“section” means a section of this Act;

“shoreline” means the shoreline at low-water mark;

“water ski” includes an aquaplane or similar device and the verb "to water ski" and its participles shall be construed accordingly.

Restrictions on Water Skiing and Driving Motor Boats

**3.** Water skiing or driving motor boats inshore prohibited.

(1) No person shall water ski or drive any motor boat, whether or not with a water skier in tow, within two hundred feet of the shoreline in any area to which, for the time being, this section applies, except when the motor boat or water skier is either —

(a) approaching or leaving a marina, dock, jetty, or other installation of a permanent nature intended for the reception of boats;

(b) proceeding at a speed not exceeding three knots and with due caution and circumspection in the most direct line consistent with safe navigation between the open sea beyond the restricted zone and the shore or any mooring within the restricted zone;

(c) towing a water skier in a lane clearly marked off with buoys and ropes of an approved pattern between the open sea beyond the restricted zone and the shore; or

(d) in distress or rendering assistance to some other person in distress. (2) The Minister may by Order exempt any person, for such time and subject to such conditions as may be specified in the Order, from the provisions of subsection (1) for the purpose of participating in any exhibition or competition organized for public entertainment.

(3) Any person who contravenes the provisions of this section shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not less than one thousand dollars and not exceeding five thousand dollars 8 .

**4.** Reckless operation of motor boats or water skis prohibited.

(1) No person shall drive a motor boat or manipulate any water ski within the territorial waters of The Bahamas —

(a) in wilful or reckless disregard for the safety of any other person or property;

(b) without due caution and circumspection; or

(c) at a speed or in a manner likely to endanger life or limb or to damage the property of or injure any person.

Any person who contravenes the provisions of this section shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not less than one thousand dollars and not exceeding five thousand dollars 9 .

Driving under the influence of drugs or liquor prohibited.

(1) No person shall drive a motor boat within the territorial waters of The Bahamas while he is under the influence of drink or a drug to such an extent as to be incapable of having proper control of the motor boat.

(2) For the purposes of this section any person who is found to be in charge of a motor boat the engine of which is running, shall be deemed to be driving that motor boat whether or not the boat is actually in motion, unless the boat is securely tied up to or at a marina, dock, jetty or wharf or to a fixed mooring, or is riding at anchor.

(3) Any person who contravenes the provisions of this section shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not less than one thousand dollars and not exceeding five thousand dollars 10 .

**6.** Safety restrictions on water skiing.

(1) It shall not be lawful for any person to participate in any way in water skiing in any area to which, for the time being, this section applies unless the following conditions are observed —

(a) every person water skiing shall wear an efficient safety floatation device;

(b) there shall be a minimum of one person of or over the age of sixteen years additional to the driver in the towing motor boat to act as a lookout;

(c) water skiing shall not take place during the hours of darkness: Provided that the Minister may in writing grant his exemption from all or any of the provisions of this section in the case of any water skiing exhibition or competition organised for public entertainment.

(2) Any person who contravenes the provisions of this section shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding one thousand dollars 11 .

**6A.** Safety restrictions on driving a jet ski. 12

(1) No person shall —

(a) operate or drive a jet ski unless that person is wearing a securely fastened personal safety flotation device approved by the Port Controller;

(b) carry another person as a passenger on a jet ski unless the passenger is wearing a securely fastened personal safety flotation device approved by the Port Controller;

drive or operate a jet ski during the hours of darkness.

A person who contravenes this section commits an offence and is 13 liable on summary conviction to a fine not less than one thousand dollars and not exceeding five thousand dollars.

*Age*

**7.** restriction on driving motor boats.

(1) Subject to the provisions of subsection (3) of this section it shall not be lawful for any person under the age of sixteen years to drive any motor boat powered with an engine rated at more than ten horse power in any area to which, for the time being, this section applies.

(2) Any person who contravenes the provisions of this section and any other person who knowingly permits a person under the age of sixteen years to contravene such provisions shall each be guilty of an offence and shall each be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding one thousand dollars 14 .

(3) This section shall not apply to any person under the age of sixteen years who has satisfied an officer appointed for that purpose by the Minister, by practical examination, that he has the knowledge, skill and ability to navigate a motor boat powered with an engine rated at more than ten horse power without danger to himself or to the public; but the burden of proof that any such person has so satisfied such officer shall in any proceedings lie upon the person charged.

(4) Notwithstanding anything contained in subsection (1) of this section a person between the ages of fourteen and sixteen years may drive any motor boat to which that subsection applies while under the supervision of a person of or over the age of sixteen years and no person shall be guilty of an offence under the provisions of this subsection by reason of his permitting such a person to do so in such circumstances.

**8.** Powers of peace officers.

(1) Any peace officer who has reason to believe that an offence has been or is being committed against section 3, 4, 5, 6 or 7, may stop and go on board any motor boat which appears to him to have been used in the commission of any such offence and may require any person whom he suspects of having committed such offence and any other person in or belonging to the motor boat to declare to him immediately that person's true name and address.

If any person refuses to declare his name or address or fails to give his true name and address when thereto required under the provisions of this section he shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding one thousand dollars 15 , and a peace officer may apprehend without warrant any person who so refuses to declare his name and address or whom such officer suspects of giving a false name and address or of intending to abscond.

16 8A. Power to detain, impound and dispose of motor boat.

(1) An authorised officer or a peace officer may, without warrant, seize and detain any motor boat where there is reasonable cause to believe that the motor boat —

(a) has been or is being operated in contravention of this Act;

(b) has been used in connection with an offence under this Act; or

(c) presents a safety risk or has violated operational directives issued by the Authority.

(2) A motor boat seized under this section may be delivered into the custody of the Authority or to such person authorised in writing by the Authority to provide secure towing, storage, or impoundment services which may be recovered from the owner of the motor boat as a civil debt.

(3) The owner of the motor boat shall be responsible for all reasonable costs incurred in connection with its seizure, towing, storage, and maintenance during the period of detention.

(4) A motor boat may be released to its owner upon —

(a) the payment in full of all outstanding fines, penalties, or costs;

(b) the remedy of any breach to the satisfaction of the Authority; or

(c) the issuance of a release order by a competent court.

(5) Where the Authority is satisfied that the motor boat was not used in connection with the commission of an offence, or that no breach of the Act occurred, the Authority shall release the motor boat and waive any associated charges.

(6) Where a fixed penalty notice has been issued in relation to a motor boat, and the owner of the motor boat disputes liability and elects to have the matter heard before a Magistrates Court, the impoundment shall continue pending the resolution of the proceedings.

(7) If the court finds in favour of the owner of the motor boat or determines that no offence was committed, the motor boat shall be released without penalty and all reasonable impoundment charges shall be waived.

(8) Where such costs and any applicable fines remain unpaid for a period exceeding sixty days from the date of seizure of a motor boat, the Authority may, after giving not less than fourteen days' written notice to the last

known owner by registered mail, or where the owner cannot be identified, by public notice, sell the motor boat by public auction or tender. The proceeds of sale of any motor boat shall be applied —

(a) firstly, to satisfy the costs of seizure, storage, and sale; and

(b) secondly, to pay any outstanding fines or penalties.

(10) Any remaining balance from the sale of a motor boat shall be returned to the owner, if known and if the owner cannot be located within ninety days after the sale, such balance shall be paid into the Consolidated Fund.

17 8B. Fixed Penalty.

(1) Where an authorised officer reasonably believes that a person has committed an offence under this Act which is triable summarily, the officer may serve upon that person a fixed penalty notice in the prescribed form offering the opportunity to discharge any liability to conviction by payment of a fixed penalty.

(2) A person who pays the fixed penalty for which he is charged shall not be liable to be prosecuted for the offence, shall not be regarded as having been convicted of the offence and the matter shall be deemed to have been fully disposed of.

(3) Where the penalty is not paid within the time specified, proceedings may be commenced in respect of the offence, and the notice shall be treated as a complaint and summons under the Criminal Procedure Code Act.

Registration of Motor Boats

**9.** Certain motor boats to be registered.

(1) No motor boat to which this section applies shall be used within any of the territorial waters of The Bahamas to which this section extends unless and until it is registered under this Act:

Provided that any motor boat which is for the time being registered under the provisions of the Boat Registration Act shall, for so long as it remains so registered, be exempt from registration under this Act. (2) Any person who uses an unregistered motor boat to which this section applies shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding one thousand dollars 18 and to an additional fine equal to twice the appropriate registration fee of that motor boat.

(3) This section applies to motor boats powered with an engine rated at more than ten horse power or exceeding fifteen feet in overall length.

**9A.** Inspection.

Subject to the provisions of subsection (3), of this section, every motor boat before being registered under this Act shall, together with the machinery, sail, gear, fixtures, equipment, apparel and appurtenances used therewith, be submitted for the inspection of the Port Controller, and the Port Controller shall decide whether such boat is fit and proper to be registered, and after registration an inspection shall take place annually.

(2) Where any alteration affecting the terms of the licence 20 is made to any boat which is registered under this Act, or to any machinery, sail, gear, fixtures, equipment, apparel or appurtenances thereof, such boat shall within fourteen days of the making of such alteration be submitted for the inspection of the Port Controller.

(3) The owner of the boat shall pay the fee specified in the Schedule for the inspection.

**10.** Procedure for registration.

(1) Every person desirous of using a motor boat to which section 9 applies shall make application for registration of that boat to the Port Controller in New Providence or to the Commissioner in an Out Island District and shall furnish the Port Controller or the Commissioner, as the case may be, with such information concerning the boat as may be prescribed.

(2) Upon application being duly made under the provisions of this section and upon payment of the appropriate fee, the Port Controller or Commissioner shall enter the prescribed particulars of the boat (which particulars 21 shall include particulars of the ownership of the boat) in a register to be maintained by him for that purpose and shall allocate a registration number to the boat.

(3) Registration shall be valid for a period of one year from the date thereof, shall expire on the thirty-first of March in the year following that of registration and may be renewed from time to time on the payment of the prescribed fee.

(4) It shall be the duty of the person registered as the owner of a boat under this section forthwith to cause the registration number thereof to be painted clearly upon both sides of the bow of the boat in the prescribed manner. Any person who uses a motor boat to which section 9 applies in any of the territorial waters to which this section extends without its correct registration number being clearly painted on both sides of the bow shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding one thousand dollars 22 .

10A.Certificate of Deletion.

The owner of a boat may request in writing the cancellation of the registration of the boat and the request shall be accompanied by —

(a) the certificate of registration of the boat;

(b) a document confirming the identity of the owner; and

(c) any other documents required by the Port Controller or the Commissioner.

(2) The Port Controller or the Commissioner, upon being satisfied that the owner of the boat has complied with subsection (1), shall cancel the registration of the boat in the register and shall issue a Certificate of Deletion on payment of the prescribed fee.

(3) The Certificate of Deletion shall be conclusive evidence of the cancellation of the boat's registration.

**11.** Notification of change of ownership or engine.

(1) Upon any change of ownership of a registered motor boat it shall be the duty of —

(a) the registered owner thereof forthwith to notify the name of the transferee to the Port Controller or to the Commissioner of the District where the boat was registered; and

(b) the transferee to apply to the Port Controller or such Commissioner for the transfer of the registration into his name.

(2) Upon the fitting of any new engine, whether or not it is rated the same as the former engine, to a registered motor boat, it shall be incumbent upon the registered owners thereof to notify the Port Controller or the Commissioner of the District where the boat was registered, and to supply such information concerning the new engine as may be prescribed.

(3) Any person who contravenes the provisions of this section shall be guilty of an offence and liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding one thousand dollars 24 .

(4) Upon being notified of any change of ownership of, or change of engine fitted to, a registered motor boat the Port Controller or Commissioner, as the case may be, shall cause the appropriate register to be duly amended in accordance with the information supplied to him.
