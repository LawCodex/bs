### REFERENDUM

*16 of 1977. 7 of 2002. 26 of 2012.*

An Act to provide for a framework for the conduct of a constitutional referendum or any other referendum.

[Commencement 29th December, 1977]

*Short title. 26 of 2012, s. 3.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Referendum Act.

*Interpretation. 26 of 2012, s. 4.*

**1A.** In this Act, “Minister” means the Minister responsible for Parliamentary Registration and Elections.

*Holding of referendum.*

**2.** (1) For the purpose of obtaining the approval of the electors qualified to vote in an election of members of the House of Assembly with respect to a Bill submitted to them, which Bill seeks to alter an Article of the Constitution specified in Article 54(2) or (3) of the Constitution or any of the provisions of The Bahamas Independence Act, 1973, a vote shall be taken by way of a referendum held in accordance with this Act.

*7 of 2002, s. 3. 26 of 2012, s. 5.*

(2) The Governor-General may by proclamation appoint a day for the holding of a constitutional referendum, being a day not earlier than twenty-one days after the date of the proclamation.

*7 of 2002, s. 4. 26 of 2012, s. 5.*

(3) The electors qualified to vote in a constitutional referendum shall be the electors who are qualified to vote as electors for the Election of members of the House of Assembly and for the purpose of constitutional referendum the register of voters prepared pursuant to section 13 of the Parliamentary Elections Act, shall be deemed to be closed ten days before the holding of the constitutional referendum.

*Question to be asked in referendum. 26 of 2012, s. 6.*

**3.** The question to be asked in a constitutional referendum shall be set out in the Bill which seeks to amend the Constitution or The Bahamas Independence Act, 1973, as the case may be.

*Holding of any other referendum.*

**3A.** (1) Where the Minister considers it desirable that the views of the electorate of The Bahamas be obtained on any matter of national importance (other than a matter to which section 2(1) applies) he may advise the Governor-General to issue an Order directing that a referendum other than a constitutional referendum be held for that purpose.

*CH.1 – 4*

(2) The question to be asked in a referendum (other than a constitutional referendum under section 2), and the form of the ballot paper to be used for that purpose, shall be as set out in an Order prescribed in the regulations.

*Ch. 7.*

(3) The electors qualified to vote in a referendum shall be the electors who are qualified to vote as electors for the election of members of the House of Assembly and for the purposes of such referendum the register of voters prepared pursuant to section 13 of the Parliamentary Elections Act, shall be deemed to be closed fifteen days before the holding of the referendum.

(4) The Governor-General on the advice of the Minister, by Order published in the Gazette appoint a day for the holding of a referendum other than a constitutional referendum.

*Conduct of referendum. 26 of 2012, s. 7. Ch. 7.*

**3B.** (1) The functions which, in relation to a general election, are conferred on the Parliamentary Commissioner by any provision of the Parliamentary Elections Act, shall, in relation to any referendum under this Act, be discharged by that officer.

(2) The Parliamentary Commissioner shall, inaddition to the functions conferred upon him by subsection (1), be responsible for conducting the count of votes cast in any referendum under this Act and for certifying the total of the ballot papers and the respective answers relating to the referendum.

(3) The Parliamentary Commissioner shall in writing appoint such persons as may be necessary —

(a) as Returning Officers, presiding officers and polling clerks for the purpose of conducting a referendum under this Act;

(b) to assist the Parliamentary Commissioner in conducting the count of votes cast in a referendum under this Act.

*Ch. 7.*

(4) The functions which, in relation to a general election are conferred respectively on returning officers and presiding officers by any provision of the Parliamentary Elections Act shall, in relation to any referendum under this Act, be discharged by those officers respectively.
