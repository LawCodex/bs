### ALIENS RESTRICTION

*3 of 1931 5 of 1987*

An Act to prohibit aliens from acting in certain capacities on ships registered in The Bahamas.

[Assent 28th May, 1931]

[Commencement 1st September, 1931]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Aliens Restriction Act.

*Restrictions as to aliens.*

1 2. No alien shall act as master of any mail or passenger ship registered in The Bahamas or as skipper or second hand of a fishing boat registered in The Bahamas, except in the case of a ship or boat employed habitually in voyages between ports outside The Bahamas:

Provided that this prohibition shall not apply to any alien who has acted as a master of a British ship, or as skipper or second hand of a British fishing boat, at any time during the War and is certified by the Admiralty to have performed good and faithful service in that capacity.

*Penalty. 5 of 1987, s. 2.*

**3.** Any person who acts in contravention of this Act or who aids and abets any person in any contravention of this Act shall be liable on summary conviction to a fine of two hundred dollars or to imprisonment for a term of six months.

Note:-The amendment of s. 2., proposed by the Aliens Restriction Amendment Act, 1939 (14 of 1939), has never come into effect.
