### HOTELS (VALIDATION OF COLLECTION OF LICENCE FEES)

### HOTELS (VALIDATION OF COLLECTION OF LICENCE FEES)

*10 of 1981*

An Act to validate collection of hotel licence fees.

[Commencement 30th December, 1981]

*10 of 1981*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Hotels (Validation of Collection of Licence Fees) Act, 1981, and shall be read and construed as one with the Hotels Act, 1970.

*Short title.*

**2.** Where before the commencement of this Act, hotel licence fees have been collected pursuant to section 15(1) notwithstanding that section 15(1) has not been brought into force and section 15(2) has not been complied with, all hotel licence fees purported to be collected in pursuance of section 15(1) shall be deemed to have been validly collected.

*Validation of hotel licence fees.*

**3.** Section 15(1) of the Hotels Act shall be deemed to have come into force on the 1st day of February, 1971 as if that date were a date validly appointed by Notice in the Gazette under subsection (2) of section 15.
