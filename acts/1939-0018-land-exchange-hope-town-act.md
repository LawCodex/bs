# Land Exchange Hope Town Act

*[IV – 67*

Act No. 18 of 1939

*18 of 1939*

LAND EXCHANGE HOPE TOWN An act to authorise the public board of works for Hope Town, Elbow Cay, Abaco, to exchange certain land at Hope Town for certain other land at Hope Town.

[Commencement 27th February, 1939]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Land Exchange Hope Town Act.

*Authorizes Hope Town Board of works to exchange certain land.*

**2.** (1) It shall be lawful for the Public Board of Works for Hope Town, Elbow Cay, Abaco, to exchange the land described in the First Schedule in this Act of certain other land in that district owned by Charles Summer Dolley described in the Second Schedule in this Act.

(2) To effect such exchange it shall be lawful for the said Board to convey the land described in the said First Schedule to the said Charles Summer Dolley in fee simple upon the said Charles Summer Dolley conveying to the said Board in fee simple the land described in the said Second Schedule.

## FIRST SCHEDULE (Section 2)

ALL that piece parcel or lot of land situate on the Settlement of Hope Town in the Island of Elbow Cay which said piece parcel or lot of land is bounded on the north by land the property of Hutchins Malone and running thereon one hundred and fifteen

(115) feet, on the east by a public road and running thereon forty-nine (49) feet, on the south by land the property of the said Charles Sumner Dolley and running thereon one hundred and twenty-three

(123) feet, and on the west by a public road and running thereon fifty (50) feet.
