**8.** Validation of collection of premium tax.

The imposition, recovery and application of fees in respect of the levy of three percent on gross premiums collected by a registered insurer or association since the 1 July, 2019, is validated and is deemed to have been lawfully, imposed, recovered and applied.

**9.** Transitional.

Notwithstanding section 4 of this Act, the Superintendent shall perform the functions of the Chairman until such time as a Chairman has been appointed.
