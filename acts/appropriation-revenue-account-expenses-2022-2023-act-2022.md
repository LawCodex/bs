## SCHEDULE 3

c EXTRAORDINARY No. 16 of 2022 OFFICIAL GAZETTE THE BAHAMAS APPROPRIATION (REVENUE ACCOUNT PUBLISHED BY AUTHORITY EXPENSES)(2022/2023) ACT, 2022 NASSAU 23 rd August, 2011

AN ACT FOR THE APPROPRIATION OF DIVERS SUMS OF MONEY FOR AND TOWARDS DEFRAYING THE EXPENSES OF THE GOVERNMENT OF THE COMMONWEALTH OF THE BAHAMAS ON REVENUE ACCOUNT FOR THE FINANCIAL YEAR COMMENCING 1ST JULY, 2022 AND ENDING 30TH JUNE, 2023

[Date of Assent – 30 th June, 2022]

Enacted Enacted by by the the Parliament Parliament of of The The Bahamas Bahamas

**1.** Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Appropriation (Revenue Account Expenses) (2022/2023) Act, 2022.

**2.** Authority for payment.

There may be paid out of the Treasury upon the authority of a warrant under the hand of the Minister of Finance during the financial year beginning on the 1 st day of July, 2022 and ending on the 30th day of June, 2023 the several sums of money for defraying the recurrent expenses on revenue account for the public services of The Bahamas set forth in the Schedule.
