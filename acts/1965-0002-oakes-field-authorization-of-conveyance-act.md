# Oakes Field (Authorization of Conveyance) Act

*IV – 262]*

Act No. 2 of 1965

### OAKES FIELD (AUTHORIZATION OF CONVEYANCE)

*2 of 1965*

An Act to empower the Minister charged with responsibility for administration of the Sports Act 1963 to convey certain lands at Oakes Field to the Treasurer.

[Commencement 18th March, 1965]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Oakes Field (Authorization of Conveyance) Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** In this Act —

*Ch. 385*

“Minister” means the Minister charged by the Governor under the provisions of the Constitution with the responsibility for the administration ofthe Sports Act.

*Minister may convey certain lands to the Treasurer. Ch. 385.*

**3.** (1) Notwithstanding anything contained in The Sports Act, it shall be lawful for the Minister to convey in fee simple to the Treasurer all or any of the tracts of land described in the Schedule to this Act, which said tracts of land are now vested in him pursuant to the provisions of sections 3(2) and 4(2) of the Sports Act aforesaid.

(2) The Minister shall convey the said tract or tracts of land by the style and title assigned to him by the Governor under the provisions of the Constitution and the official seal of the Minister shall be affixed to the conveyance of such land in execution of the same.

*Treasurer shall hold land in trust.*

**4.** All land conveyed to the Treasurer in manner provided by section 3 of this Act shall be and remain vested in him in fee simple and the Treasurer shall hold the same in trust for Her Majesty in right of Her Government of the Bahama Islands for the public purpose of The Bahamas.
