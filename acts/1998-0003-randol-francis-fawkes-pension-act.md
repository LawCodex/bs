# Randol Francis Fawkes Pension Act

*[IV – 381*

### RANDOL FRANCIS FAWKES

RANDOL FRANCIS FAWKES Act No. 3 of 1998

### RANDOL FRANCIS FAWKES

## ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS

### SECTION

**1.** Short title.

**2.** Pension.

**3.** Widow’s allowance.

**4.** Charge on Consolidated Fund.

*3 of 1998*

An Act to provide for a pension for Randol Francis Fawkes.

[Assent 30th April, 1998]

[Commencement 5th May, 1998]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Randol Francis Fawkes Pension Act, 1998.

*Pension.*

**2.** Notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained in any other Act relating to the grant of pensions and gratuities, a pension is granted to Randol Francis Fawkes in the amount of fifteen thousand dollars per annum payable by equal monthly instalments.

*Widow’s allowance.*

**3.** (1) Where Randol Francis Fawkes dies leaving a widow she shall be paid during her lifetime an annual allowance equivalent to two thirds of the pension to which Randol Francis Fawkes would have been entitled.

(2) A widow’s annual allowance under this section shall be payable in equal monthly instalments as far as possible.

*Charge on Consolidated Fund.*

**4.** There is charged on and paid out of the Consolidated Fund all such sums of money as may be granted by way of pension and allowance in accordance with this Act.
