# Western Cemetery Act

*[IV – 31*

### WESTERN CEMETRY

WESTERN CEMETRY Act No. 3 of 1906

*3 of 1906*

WESTERN CEMETERY An Act to alter the name of Potter’s Field Burial Ground to the Western Cemetery.

[Commencement 29th May, 1906]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Western Cemetery Act.

*Potter’s Field Burial Ground to be known as The Western Cemetery.*

**2.** The burial ground on Augusta Street in the Island of New Providence and now known as Potter’s Field Burial Ground shall, from and after the passing of this Act, be known as The Western Cemetery and all references to such burial ground in any Act of Assembly or any rule, regulation or order having the force and effect of law under the name of Potter’s Field Burial Ground shall be read and construed as though in every such instance the words “The Western Cemetery” were therein substituted in lieu of the words “Potter’s Field Burial Ground.”

*THE BAHAMAS*
