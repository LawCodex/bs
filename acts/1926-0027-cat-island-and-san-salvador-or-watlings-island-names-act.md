# Cat Island and San Salvador or Watlings Island Names Act

*IV – 40]*

### CAT ISLAND AND SAN SALVADOR OR WATLINGS

CAT ISLAND AND SAN SALVADOR OR WATLINGS ISLAND NAMES Act No. 27 of 1926

*27 of 1926*

CAT ISLAND AND SAN SALVADOR OR WATLINGS ISLAND NAMES An Act relating to the names of the islands generally known as San Salvador and Watlings Island.

[Commencement 6th May, 1926]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Cat Island and San Salvador or Watlings Island Names Act.

*Renaming of Islands.*

**2.** From and after the coming into force of the Act:

(a) The Island now generally known as San Salvador or Cat Island shall be called and referred to as Cat Island; and

(b) The Island now generally known as Watlings Island shall be called and referred to as San Salvador or Watlings Island.

*Reference in previous Acts, etc.*

**3.** Wheresoever in any Act, Order in Council or rule passed or official notice published before the coming into force of this Act reference is made:

(a) To the Island of San Salvador the same shall unless the context otherwise requires be con-strued to mean Cat Island; and

(b) to Watlings or Watlings Island the same shall unless the context otherwise requires be con-strued to mean San Salvador or Watlings Island.
