# Roman Catholic Vicar Forane Incorporation Act

*IV – 32]*

Act No. 21 of 1911

*21 of 1911 9 of 1912 26 of 1925*

ROMAN CATHOLIC VICAR FORANE INCORPORATION An Act for the incorporation of the Roman Catholic Vicar Forane in The Bahamas.

[Commencement 13th July, 1911]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Roman Catholic Vicar Forane Incorporation Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** In this Act the term “successors in office” means the person from time to time having, by virtue of the Canon Law of the Roman Catholic Church or by appointment by the Roman Catholic Archbishop of New York in the United States of America or by appointment by the proper authority in Rome, supreme ecclesiastical jurisdiction in The Bahamas in the Roman Catholic Church.

*Roman Catholic Vicar Forane to be a body corporate.*

**3.** The Very Reverend Chrysostom Schreiner, O.S.B., the Roman Catholic Vicar Forane in The Bahamas, and his successors in office, shall be a body corporate and have the name of “The Roman Catholic Vicar Forane in the Bahamas,” and by that name shall have perpetual succession and shall have full power to purchase, take, hold, enjoy, sell, grant, convey, mortgage, lease, invest, and otherwise deal with and dispose of, all property real and personal of what nature or kind soever hereby vested and hereafter acquired and may sue and be sued in all the Courts in The Bahamas.

*Vesting of Property held by present or any previous Archbishop of New York or Vicar Forane.*

**4.** All property in The Bahamas real or personal, of what nature or kind soever, now held by or vested in the Most Reverend John M. Farley Roman Catholic Archbishop of New York or the Very Reverend Chrysostom Schreiner, O.S.B., Roman Catholic Vicar Forane in The Bahamas or previously held by or vested in any other Roman Catholic Archbishop of New York or Roman Catholic Vicar Forane in The Bahamas, and not heretofore transferred or otherwise disposed of, whether the transfers or conveyances thereof vest the same in such

*Vesting of property bequeathed by Will to Roman Catholic Church.*

**5.** All property real or personal, of what nature or kind soever, heretofore and hereafter devised or be-queathed by will, or otherwise given to the Roman Catholic Church in The Bahamas, shall be held by, and is and shall be hereby vested in, and shall be deemed to be the the property of, the said corporate body.
