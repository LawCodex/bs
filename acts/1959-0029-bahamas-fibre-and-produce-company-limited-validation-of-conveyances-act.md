# Bahamas Fibre and Produce Company, Limited, Validation of Conveyances Act

VALIDATION OF CONVEYANCES Act No. 29 of 1959

*29 of 1959*

BAHAMAS FIBRE AND PRODUCE COMPANY, LIMITED, VALIDATION OF CONVEYANCES An Act to validate certain conveyances of land situate in the Island of New Providence made on behalf of The Bahamas Fibre and Produce Company, Limited.

[Commencement 13th July, 1959]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as The Bahamas Fibre and Produce Company, Limited, Validation of Conveyances Act.

*Validation of certain Conveyances.*

**2.** The several indentures of conveyances (hereinafter referred to as “the said indentures”) brief particulars of which are set out in the Schedule hereto are hereby made effective for all purposes and they shall be deemed to have conveyed to the purchasers in the said indentures respectively named all the estate, right title, interest, claim and demand of The Bahamas Fibre and Produce Company, Limited (a Company incorporated under the laws of the Bahama Islands which was removed by the Registrar General from the Register of Companies under the Removal of Defunct Companies Act on the 14th day of June, 1926) of and in the hereditaments described in and purported to be conveyed by the said indentures respectively.

*Commencement.*

**3.** So far as this Act relates to each of the said indentures it shall be deemed to have come into operation on the date of each such indenture.

*THE BAHAMAS*
