# Leonard Joseph Knowles Pension Act

*IV – 382]*

### LEONARD JOSEPH KNOWLES

LEONARD JOSEPH KNOWLES Act No. 21 of 1998

### LEONARD JOSEPH KNOWLES

## ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS

### SECTION

**1.** Short title.

**2.** Pension.

**3.** Widow’s allowance.

**4.** Charge on Consolidated Fund.

*21 of 1998*

An Act to provide for a pension for Leonard Joseph Knowles.

[Assent 9th July, 1998]

[Commencement 16th July, 1998]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Leonard Joseph Knowles Pension Act, 1998.

*Pension.*

**2.** Notwithstanding anything to the contrary con-tained in any other Act relating to the grant of pensions and gratuities, a pension is granted to Leonard Joseph Knowles in the amount of fifteen thousand dollars per annum payable by equal monthly instalments.

*Widow’s allowance.*

**3.** (1) Where Leonard Joseph Knowles dies leaving a widow, she shall be paid during her lifetime an annual allowance equivalent to two thirds of the pension to which Leonard Joseph Knowles would have been entitled.

(2) A widow’s annual allowance under this section shall be payable in equal monthly instalments as far as possible.

*Charge on Consolidated Fund.*

**4.** There is charged on and paid out of the Consolidated Fund all such sums of money as may be granted by way of pension and allowance in accordance with this Act.
