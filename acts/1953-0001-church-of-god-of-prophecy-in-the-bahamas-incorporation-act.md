# Church of God of Prophecy in the Bahamas Incorporation Act

*[IV – 121*

INCORPORATION Act No. 1 of 1953

*1 of 1953*

CHURCH OF GOD OF PROPHECY IN THE BAHAMAS INCORPORATION An Act to incorporate the trustees of the Church of God of Prophecy in The Bahamas.

[Assent 2nd March, 1953]

[Commencement 30th June, 1953]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Church of God of Prophecy in the Bahamas Incorporation Act.

*Present Trustees.*

**2.** Alvin Simon Moss, Martin Luther Rolle, Hermis Abiel Ferguson, Earnel Derrington Adderley, Dexton John Brown and Huel Ferguson shall be deemed to be the present Trustees of The Church of God of Prophecy in the Bahamas.

*Present Trustees and their successors in office to be a body corporate.*

**3.** The Trustees of The Church of God of Prophecy in the Bahamas and their successors in office shall be deemed to be a body corporate and have the name of The Trustees of The Church of God of Prophecy in the Bahamas and by that name shall have perpetual succession and shall have full power to purchases, take, hold, enjoy, sell, grant, convey mortgage, lease, invest and otherwise deal with and dispose of all property real and personal of what nature or kind soever vested in or acquired by the said body corporate and may sue or be sued in all courts of the Bahamas.

*Vesting of property at present held in trust.*

**4.** All property in the Bahamas real or personal of what nature or kind soever now vested in or held in a manner by any person in trust for or for the use and benefit of The Church of God of Prophecy in the Bahamas shall vest in and be held by the said body corporate.

*Vesting of property given to the Church.*

**5.** All property real or personal of what nature of kind soever heretofore or hereafter devised or bequeathed by will or otherwise given to The Church of God of Prophecy in the Bahamas shall vest in and by held by and shall be deemed to be the property of the said body corporate.

*Property acquired by Trustees to be held for the use and benefit of the Church.*

**6.** All property real or personal of what nature or kind soever vested in or acquired by the said body corporate under this Act shall be held by the said body corporate for the use and benefit of The Church of God of Prophecy in the Bahamas.

*Rights of the Queen and others reserved.*

**7.** Nothing in this Act shall prejudice or affect the rights of Her Majesty the Queen, Her Heirs and Successors or of any other person or persons except such as are mentioned in this Act and those claiming by, from, through or under them.
