# Charles Francis Christie Real Estate Act

*[IV – 45*

Act No. 24 of 1931

*24 of 1931*

CHARLES FRANCIS CHRISTIE REAL ESTATE An Act vesting certain real estate in Charles Francis Christie.

[Assent 28th May, 1931]

*Ch. 308.*

[Commencement 8th August, 1931] WHEREAS a certain lot of land situate in the Eastern District of New Providence and hereinafter particularly described is in accordance with the provisions of section eight of the Removal of Defunct Companies Act now vested in the Treasurer subject to the disposition of the Legislature:

and WHEREAS It is desirable that the said lot of land should vest in the said Charles Francis Christie the heir-at-law and personal representative of the late George Francis Christie.

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Charles Francis Christie Real Estate Act.

*Certain real estate to vest in Charles Francis Christie.*

**2.** From and after the coming into operation of this Act the land referred to and described in the Schedule to this Act shall be and the same is hereby vested in the said Charles Francis Christie, the heir-at-law and personal representative of the said late George Francis Christie, in fee simple.
