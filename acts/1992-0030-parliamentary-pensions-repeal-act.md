## ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS

### SECTION

**1.** Short title.

**2.** Refund of contributions.

*30 of 1992*

An Act to repeal the Parliamentary Pensions Act, 1989.

[Assent 2nd October, 1992]

[Commencement 9th February, 1994]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Parliamentary Pensions (Repeal) Act, 1992.

*Refund of contributions.*

**2.** Where any person has made contributions pursuant to the provisions of the Parliamentary Pensions Act, 1989, there shall forthwith be refunded to him an amount equal to the total amount of the said contributions, together with interest at the rate of eight per centum per annum; and, for the purposes of the calculation and the payment of such refund, the provisions of the said Act shall, notwithstanding such repeal, continue to have full force and effect.
