# Finance (Supplementation and Variation of Appropriation)(Capital Development)(2010) Act, 2011

No. 7 of2011

### FINANCE (SUPPLEMENATION AND VARIATION OF APPROPRIATION) (CAPITAL DEVELOPMENT)

### (2010) ACT, 2011

AN ACT TO SUPPLEMENT AND VARY THE APPROPRIATION OF THE SUM THE ISSUE OF WHICH WAS AUTHORIZED BY THE APPROPRIATION (CAPITAL DEVELOPMENT) (2010/2011) ACT, 2010

[Date of Assent - 1 sr April, 201 1] Enacted by the Parliament of The Bahamas

**1.** Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Finance (Supplementation and Variation of Appropriation) (Capital Development) (20 I 0) Act, 2011.

Construction of Act No.10 of 2010. 2.

In this Act and in any other written law, a reference to the Appropriation (Capital Development) (20 I 0/2011) Act, 2010 ("the Appropriation Act, 20 10") shall be construed as a reference to that Act as for the time being varied or otherwise amended by this Act.

**3.** Increase in Head of Expenditure.

The sums appropriated to the Head of Expenditure set out in the First Column of

Part I of the Schedule to this Act by the Appropriation Act, 20 I 0 are increased by the amounts specified in the Second Column of that Prut.
