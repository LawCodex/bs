# Public Boards (Divesting of Title) Act

*[IV – 259*

### PUBLIC BOARDS (DIVESTING OF TITLE)

PUBLIC BOARDS (DIVESTING OF TITLE) Act No. 12 of 1964

*12 of 1964*

PUBLIC BOARDS (DIVESTING OF TITLE) An Act to vest in the Treasurer all land vested in the Agricultural and Marine Products Board and the Telecommunications Board.

[Commencement 25th June, 1964]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Public Boards (Divesting of Title) Act.

*Divest public boards of title to land.*

**2.** It is hereby declared that all land which immediately prior to the seventh day of January 1964 was vested in the Agricultural and Marine Products Board and the Telecommunications Board shall with effect from that date be deemed to be vested in the Treasurer to be held by him in trust for Her Majesty in right of Her Government of the Bahama Islands for public purposes.
