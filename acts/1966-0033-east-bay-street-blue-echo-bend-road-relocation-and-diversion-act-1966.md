# East Bay Street (Blue Echo Bend) Road Relocation and Diversion Act 1966

*IV – 278]*

### EAST BAY STREET (BLUE ECHO BEND) ROAD

EAST BAY STREET (BLUE ECHO BEND) ROAD RELOCATION AND DIVERSION Act No. 33 of 1966

*EAST*

*An 33 of 1966*

*of a New*

BAY STREET (BLUE ECHO BEND) ROAD RELOCATION AND DIVERSION Act to authorise the relocation and diversion certain portion of East Bay Street in the Island of Providence.

[Commencement 5th October, 1966]

*Short title.*

*Echo*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the East Bay Street (Blue Bend) Road Relocation and Diversion Act 1966.

*Interpretation. sible for E.L.A.O., 1974.*

**2.** In this Act “Minister” means the Minister respon-Public Works.

*Authorises road shall be closure and conveyance.*

**3.** Notwithstanding the provisions of any other Act, it lawful for —

(a) the Minister to authorise the relocation and diversion of a certain portion of East Bay Street in the vicinity commonly known as ‘Blue Echo Bend’;

(b) the Minister to stop up and close those portions of East Bay Street in the vicinity commonly known as “Blue Echo Bend” which portions are delineated and coloured Pink on the Survey Plan filed with a copy of this Act in the Department of Public Works and designated on the said plan as

“Area C”, “Area E” and “Area F”;

(c) the Treasurer after the stopping up and closing by the Minister of the portions of East Bay Street referred to in paragraph (b) of this section, to sell and convey the said portions to such purchaser or purchasers as may be approved by the Ministers.

*Cesser of public stopped right-of-way.*

**4.** Upon the said portions of East Bay Street being up and closed as aforesaid the right of the public to the use thereof shall cease and be extinguished.
