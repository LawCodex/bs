[Date of Assent- 16th March, 2010)

Enacted by the Parliament of The Bahamas.

**1.** Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Finance (Supplementation and Variation of Appropriation) (Capital Development) (2009) Act, 2010.

**2.** Construction of Act No.17 of 2009.

In this Act and in any other written Jaw, a reference to the Appropriation (Capital Development) (2009/2010) Act, 2009 ("the Appropriation Act, 2009") shall be construed as a reference to that Act as for the time being varied or otherwise amended by this Act.
