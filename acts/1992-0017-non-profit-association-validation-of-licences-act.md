## ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS

### SECTION

**1.** Short title.

**2.** Validation of licences.

**3.** Acts valid.

*17 of 1992*

An Act to validate certain licences issued under section 78 of the Companies Act.

[Assent 25th May, 1992]

[Commencement 1st June, 1992]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Non-Profit Association (Validation of Licences) Act, 1992.

*Validation of licences. Ch. 308.*

**2.** Any licence issued during the period 19th January, 1989 to 31st December, 1991, under section 78 of the Companies Act, shall be deemed to be validly issued, notwithstanding the fact that such a licence was not issued by the Minister responsible for Companies.

*Acts valid.*

**3.** Any act done by, or transaction carried out with a company, or its agents or servants, being a company in respect of which a licence referred to in section 2 was issued, shall be valid, notwithstanding the fact that the licence was issued by a Minister other than the Minister responsible for Companies.
