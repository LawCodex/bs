## PART IV - REGISTRATION

**17.** Qualifications for registration.

(1) A person who, after the commencement of this Act, applies to the Board to be registered as an operator of a day-care centre or pre-school and who satisfies the Board that —

(a) he can read, write, speak and understand the English language;

(b) he is trained in the care and education of children by an institution approved for that purpose by the Minister; and

(c) he is a fit and proper person to become an operator of a day-care centre or pre-school in The Bahamas, is entitled, upon compliance with this Act and on payment of the prescribed fee, to be registered under this Act.

For the purposes of subsection (1), a person is qualified to be registered as the operator of a day-care centre or pre-school if that person has not been disqualified or suspended from practising as a day-care centre or preschool operator whether within or outside The Bahamas and —

(a) has passed the prescribed examination set by the Board, or holds a degree, diploma, membership licence, certificate or other status or form of recognition granted by a University, College or body which in the opinion of the Board is evidence of satisfactory training in that profession; or

(b) has satisfied —

(i) the Board that for a period of not less than ten years he has acquired practical experience in the area of early childhood care; and

(ii) the Board that he is a citizen of The Bahamas or a permanent resident of The Bahamas whose permanent residence certificate permits him to engage in gainful occupation. (3) An application for registration under this Act shall be made in the form approved by the Board, and an applicant shall provide to the Board —

(a) evidence of his qualifications;

(b) proof of his identity;

(c) evidence that he has not been convicted of any offence of which violence, sexual abuse or dishonesty is an element; and

(d) such further or other information as the Board requires in respect of the matters specified under paragraphs (a) and (c) of subsection (1). (4) An application made under this section by a person who is not a citizen of The Bahamas, shall not be granted without evidence of a valid work permit issued under the Immigration Act.

(5) The Board shall, in respect of the registration of a person under this section, issue to that person a certificate of registration.

(6) Where the Board refuses to approve the registration of a person under this Act, the Board shall within seven days from the date of such refusal give written notice to the applicant stating the reasons for the refusal.

**18.** Registration of day-care centres and pre-schools.

(1) No person shall operate a day-care centre or pre-school unless it is registered under this Act.

(2) The Board may, upon application in the prescribed manner and —

(a) on being satisfied that the premises on which the day-care centre or pre-school would be operating is fit for that purpose;

on being satisfied that all emergency, medical and disaster plans are in place in the day-care centre or pre-school;

(c) on being satisfied that the day-care centre or pre-school would operate in a manner that is in the best interest of the public’s safety; and

(d) upon payment of the prescribed fee, grant to the applicant a certificate of registration in the prescribed form to use a building as a day-care centre or pre-school.

(3) In the case of an application made under subsection (2), the Board shall be satisfied that —

(a) the applicant is eighteen years of age or over;

(b) the applicant is not by reason of age or otherwise incapable of operating a day-care centre or pre-school;

(c) the applicant is a fit and proper person, that is —

(i) the applicant has not been convicted of any offence against this Act, or any offence of which violence, sexual abuse or dishonesty is an element;

(ii) in the case of a company, none of its directors or in the case of another body of persons, none of its members, have been convicted of any offence referred to under this paragraph;

(iii) the Board is satisfied as to the character or antecedents or competence of the applicant; and

(iv) the Board is satisfied as to the financial position of the applicant; and

(d) emergency medical and disaster plans approved by the Ministry of Health are in place in relation to the day-care centre or pre-school. (4) The Board may refuse an application for registration, if the Board is satisfied that —

(a) the applicant or a person to be employed by the applicant does not satisfy any of the provisions of subsection (3) and section 17; or

(b) the premises to be used for the operation of a day-care centre or a pre-school are not fit to be so used.

(5) Where the Board refuses an application for registration, the Board shall inform the applicant in writing and state the reason for the refusal.

**19.** Registration of existing day-care centres and pre-schools.

(1) A person who, on the date of commencement of this Act, is the owner or operator of a day-care centre or pre-school shall apply in the prescribed manner within three months after that date —

(a) to be registered as an operator of a day-care centre or pre-school; and

to have that day-care or pre-school registered pursuant to this Act. (2) Every application under this section shall be made in writing and shall include the following particulars —

(a) the name of the owner of the day-care centre or pre-school;

(b) the address and location of the day-care centre or pre-school;

(c) the name of the person having control of or in charge of the day-care centre or pre-school if different from the owner;

(d) the number of employees employed in the day-care centre or preschool as at the date of the application; and

(e) the type of training received in the care and education of children by the owner and employees who care for the children.

(3) Where an application is made under to subsection (1), the operation of the day-care centre or pre-school to which it relates may be continued pending the determination of the application.

(4) The Board shall within thirty days after the receipt of an application made under subsection (1), make a determination in respect of that application and if the applicant is qualified for registration under section 17, the applicant shall be registered.

**20.** Provisional certificate of registration.

(1) Where it appears to the Board that an applicant for registration is not qualified under section 17, the Board may, upon payment of the prescribed fee issue to that person a provisional certificate subject to such conditions as the Board may determine which shall entitle him to operate a day-care centre or pre-school as he would have been entitled to do if he had been registered in accordance with this Act.

(2) A provisional certificate shall, unless it is canceled, remain in force for a period not exceeding six months.

(3) The Board may, at any time, cancel a provisional certificate of registration.

**21.** Certificate of registration.

(1) A certificate of registration issued under this Act shall, unless suspended or revoked, be valid for one year from the date of issue.

(2) Application for renewal of a certificate of registration shall be made not later than thirty days before the date of expiration.

If a certificate of registration has been lost, destroyed or defaced, the Board may issue a replacement upon payment of the prescribed fee.

Certificate to be displayed.

*A shall*

certificate of registration issued in respect of a day-care centre or pre-school be displayed in a conspicuous place in that day-care centre or pre-school.

**23.** Employees of day-care centres and pre-schools.

(1) No person shall employ another person as a care giver in a day-care centre or pre-school unless that person is —

(a) certified annually by a medical officer to be in good health and free from communicable diseases;

(b) eighteen years of age or over;

(c) a fit and proper person, that is, that person has not been convicted of any offence of which violence, sexual abuse or dishonesty is an element; and

(d) capable of reading and writing English.

(2) No person shall employ another person in a day-care centre or pre-school for the purpose of caring for the children in a day-care centre or preschool, unless the person has received training in early childhood care and education as prescribed by the Minister.

(3) An owner or operator who operates a day-care centre or pre-school who employs a person who has not been certified by a medical officer to be in good health or who has not been trained in early childhood care and education commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction —

(a) to a fine of five thousand dollars; and

(b) in the case of a continuing offence, a further fine not exceeding one hundred dollars for each day on which the offence continues.

**24.** Register of day-care centres and pre-schools to be kept.

(1) The Board shall cause the Executive Director to keep a register of —

(a) day-care centres and pre-schools; and

(b) owners and operators of day-care centres and pre-schools. (2) The register shall contain such particulars relating to day-care centres, and pre-schools, and owners and operators as the Board may determine.

Records of day-care centres and pre-schools to be kept.

A person who operates a day-care centre or pre-school shall keep proper records showing the business conducted by him in respect of the day-care centre or preschool and shall keep such other records as may be prescribed.

**26.** Publication of day-care centres and pre-schools.

(1) The Board shall cause the register under section 24 to be published in the Gazette in each year as soon as practicable after the 31st day of January, and the 31st day of July.

(2) The Board shall keep the register open at all reasonable times for inspection by members of the public.

**27.** Penalty for operating a day-care centre or pre-school without being registered.

A person who operates a day-care centre or pre-school without being registered under this Act commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction —

(a) to a fine of five thousand dollars; and

(b) in the case of a continuing offence, a further fine not exceeding one hundred dollars for each day on which the offence continues.

**28.** Penalty for failure to pay annual registration fee.

(1) A person required under section 18 to pay an annual renewal registration fee who fails to pay such fee during the month of January in any year shall, in respect of his registration for that year pay to the Commission, in addition to the annual registration fee, a sum equal to that fee. (2) If the owner or operator of a day-care centre or pre-school fails to comply with subsection (1), the day-care centre or pre-school shall be deemed unregistered.

**29.** Notice of failure to comply with Act.

If the Board is of the opinion that an owner or operator of a day-care centre or pre-school has failed or is failing to operate a day-care centre or pre-school in accordance with this Act, the Board may give written notice specifying the noncompliance.

(1) The Board may at any time, by order suspend or revoke as the case may be, the certificate of registration of a day-care centre or pre-school —

(a) if the respective holder of the certificate of registration — has been convicted of an offence under this Act;

(iv) is convicted of a criminal offence; or

(b) if in the opinion of the Board, the day-care centre or pre-school building is unsafe or is kept in an unsanitary condition or without proper fire protection; or

(c) if the business of the day-care centre or pre-school is conducted in a manner contrary to this Act, or in such a manner that the revocation of the certificate of registration is required in the interest of public health or public interest.

(2) Before making an order under subsection (1), the Board shall give to the owner or operator a minimum of fourteen days written notice of its intention to make the order.

(3) The notice referred to under subsection (2), shall state the grounds for making the order and provide for any objections.

(4) If within fourteen days after receipt of the notice, the owner or operator notifies the Board in writing that he wishes to be heard, the Board shall before making the order, give him the opportunity to show cause in person or by representative as to why the order should not be made.

(5) If the Board after giving the owner or operator an opportunity to be heard, decides to suspend or revoke the certificate of registration, the Board shall make an order to that effect and shall send a copy of the order to the person registered.

**31.** Appeal.

(1) A person aggrieved by a decision of the Board in refusing an application for registration or renewal of registration or suspending or revoking registration may, within fourteen days after the date on which the notice of such decision was received by him, appeal against such decision to the Appeal Tribunal appointed by the Minister in accordance with the Second Schedule.

(2) An appeal under subsection (1) to the Appeal Tribunal shall not operate as a stay of the decision of the Board.

## PART V - ENFORCEMENT

Appointment of inspectors.

(1) The Minister may, after consultation with the Board, appoint officers with early childhood education to be inspectors.

(2) An inspector may enter and inspect any day-care centre or pre-school —

(a) if there is reasonable grounds to believe that the day-care centre or pre-school is being operated in contravention of this Act; or

(b) for the purposes of —

(i) investigating complaints concerning the operation of any day-care centre or pre-school, or

(ii) annual inspection of a day-care centre or pre-school to determine compliance with this Act.

(3) An inspector or person assigned to assist the inspector who has a direct or indirect pecuniary interest in the day-care centre or pre-school shall, as soon as possible, disclose the nature of his interest to the Board, and that inspector or person shall not be permitted to inspect the day-care centre or pre-school for which he has a pecuniary interest.

(4) An inspector shall be issued a certificate of identity and the inspector shall produce the certificate on entering the day-care centre or pre-school. (5) An inspector may —

(a) require the production of records or other documents required to be kept pursuant to this Act; and

(b) make copies of such records or documents.

**33.** Obligation of staff to cooperate with inspectors.

(1) An operator and employee of a day-care centre or pre-school shall cooperate with an inspector in the execution of his duties under section

32.

(2) A person who fails to comply with subsection (1) commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction —

(a) to a fine of five thousand dollars; and

(b) in the case of a continuing offence, to a further fine not exceeding one hundred dollars for each day on which the offence continues.

**34.** Offence to obstruct inspectors.

*An*

owner, operator or any other person who —

(a) obstructs the inspector in the exercise of his duties;

refuses to provide information or produce any document when required to do so by the inspector; or

(c) knowingly or intentionally supplies false or misleading information to the inspector,

commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction —

(i) to a fine of five thousand dollars; and

(ii) in the case of a continuing offence, a further fine not exceeding one hundred dollars for each day on which the offence continues.

**35.** Power of Minister to suspend operations.

(1) Notwithstanding sections 29 and 30, the Minister acting on the advice of the Board that a day-care centre or pre-school is operating in a manner that is detrimental to the public health, public safety or in the public interest, or in a manner likely to endanger the lives or health of children, may by order published in the Gazette suspend with immediate effect the operation of that day-care centre or pre-school.

(2) The Minister shall within seventy-two hours after suspending the operation of the day-care centre or pre-school, inform the owner or operator of the day-care centre or pre-school, of any conditions that must be complied with prior to the lifting of any order made under subsection (1).

(3) A person who fails to comply with an order made under subsection (1) commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine of ten thousand dollars or to imprisonment for a term of three years or to both. (4) A person aggrieved by a decision of the Minister made under subsection (1), may appeal to the Appeal Tribunal, but such appeal shall not operate as a stay of the decision of the Minister.
