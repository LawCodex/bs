### FOREIGN SERVICE ACT, 2025

AN ACT TO MAKE PROVISION FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE DEPARTMENT OF FOREIGN SERVICE; THE ADMINISTRATION OF THE FOREIGN SERVICE; THE ESTABLISHMENT OF THE BAHAMAS ALRAE RAMSEY INSTITUTE OF FOREIGN AFFAIRS AND RELATED PURPOSES.

[Date of Assent - 1 st May, 2025] Enacted by the Parliament of The Bahamas

**1.** Short title and commencement.

(1) This Act shall be cited as the Foreign Service Act, 2025.

(2) This Act shall come into operation on such date as may be appointed by the Minister by notice published in the Gazette.

**2.** Interpretation.

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

“Department” means the Department of Foreign Service established under section 3;

“Director General” means the Director General appointed to head the Department of Foreign Service referred to in section 5;

“foreign service” means the Department of Foreign Service, the administration of which is the responsibility of the Director General, comprised of diplomatic and non-diplomatic staff members of the Department serving at headquarters or a mission;

“Foreign Service Appeal Tribunal” means the appellate body established

“Foreign Service Committee” means the advisory body established

“foreign service employee” means a non-diplomatic staff member of the Department, serving at headquarters or a mission, in the capacity of —

(a) administrative and technical staff;

(b) household staff, maintenance staff, custodial staff and other service staff;

(c) staff locally employed at a mission;

“foreign service officer” means a diplomatic staff member of the Department serving at headquarters or a mission in the capacity of a diplomatic or consular officer;

“Foreign Service Orders” means the Orders referred to in section 14;

“head of mission” means the person for the time being in charge of a mission and includes the holder of the office of —

(a) Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary or High Commissioner Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary;

(b) Ambassador or High Commissioner;

(c) principal representative or permanent representative of The Bahamas accredited to any other country or an international organisation;

(d) Charge d'Affaires; and

(e) Consul-General;

“headquarters” means the head office of the Department, located within The Bahamas;

“Minister” means the Minister responsible for Foreign Affairs;

“Ministry” means the Ministry of Foreign Affairs;

“mission” includes—

(a) a diplomatic or consular office of The Bahamas overseas; and

(b) the following premises —

(i) the buildings or parts of buildings, and the land ancillary to the buildings, used for the mission, irrespective of ownership;

(ii) the residence of the head of mission.

**3.** Establishment of the Department of Foreign Service.

(1) There is established, the Department of Foreign Service for the administration of the foreign service.

The Department shall be a body corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal and may —

(a) acquire, hold and dispose of real and personal property;

(b) sue and be sued in its corporate name;

(c) enter into contracts; and

(d) establish and operate bank accounts.

(3) The Department shall include the offices listed in the Second Schedule and the Minister may, by Order, amend the Second Schedule.

(4) After the commencement of this Act, foreign service officers and foreign service employees of the Department shall not be public officers for the purposes of Article 127(c) of the Constitution.

(5) The Minister may give general and specific directions to the Director General relating to foreign relations and the conduct of the affairs of the Department.

**4.** Funds of Department.

(1) The funds and resources of the Department shall consist of —

(a) such sums as may be voted for the purposes of the Department by Parliament;

(b) such sums arising from grants, donations and other receipts from persons including national and international bodies for the purposes of education, training, and professional development of diplomatic officers and staff of the Department; and

(c) all fees and other sums which may in any manner become payable to the Department in respect of any matter incidental to its functions.

(2) There shall be paid from the funds of the Department —

(a) salaries, grants, allowances, loans, pensions of the officers and staff of the Department;

(b) such remuneration, reasonable travelling and subsistence allowances as the Committee may determine for members of the Committee when engaged in the business of the Department; and

(c) any other expenses incurred by the Department in the performance of its functions.

(3) The Director General shall be the principal accounting officer of the Department for the purposes of the Public Finance Management Act, 2023 (No. 4 of 2023).

Director General.

There shall be a Director General who shall be the head of the

(2) The Director General shall be appointed by the Minister and has responsibility for —

(a) all technical matters related to the foreign service including the formulation, promotion and advancement of foreign policy; and

(b) advising the Minister on all matters relating to the foreign service. (3) The Director General —

(a) serves at headquarters and has the rank of Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary ; and

(b) reports directly to the Minister.

(4) The academic and professional qualifications, training and experience required for appointment as Director General shall be prescribed in the Foreign Service Orders.

(5) The powers, duties and functions of the Director General are set out in the First Schedule.

**6.** Qualifications and hierarchy of diplomatic and consular officers. (1) Members of the foreign service who serve as diplomatic and consular officers must —

(a) be citizens of The Bahamas; and

(b) possess such qualifications and experience as may be prescribed in the Foreign Service Orders.

(2) The foreign service is headed by the Minister and, in order of seniority —

(a) the Director General;

(b) foreign service officers, with such hierarchy and responsibility in relation to their offices as prescribed in the Foreign Service Orders; and

(c) foreign service employees, with such hierarchy and responsibility in relation to their employment as prescribed in the Foreign Service Orders and, where applicable, their contracts of employment.

**7.** Appointment to the foreign service.

(1) The appointment of a person to the foreign service as an Ambassador, High Commissioner or any other principal representative of The Bahamas in any other country, or accredited to any international organization shall be made by the Governor-General acting in accordance with the advice of the Prime Minister in accordance with Article 111 of the Constitution.

The appointment of a person on transfer as an officer required to reside outside The Bahamas for the proper discharge of his functions other than as a head of mission shall be made by the Prime Minister in accordance with Article 112 of the Constitution.

(3) The appointment of a person as an officer serving at headquarters, shall be made by the Minister after consultation with the Foreign Service Committee.

**8.** Foreign Service Committee.

(1) There is established for the purposes of this Act, a body to be known as the Foreign Service Committee.

(2) The Foreign Service Committee is an advisory body with responsibility to advise and make recommendations to the Prime Minister and the Minister respectively in relation to the appointments referred to in section 7. (3) The Foreign Service Committee shall have power to hear, in respect of a posting, promotion, transfer or secondment of persons appointed under subsection (2)—

(a) grievances, disciplinary actions, and appeals in relation to appointments; and

(b) the review of remuneration and allowances.

(4) The members of the Foreign Service Committee shall be appointed by the Minister and are —

(a) a retired career diplomat at ambassadorial level;

(b) Chairman of the Public Service Commission or a representative;

(c) a certified human resources professional, from the public or private sector of The Bahamas;

(d) a counsel and attorney-at-law with knowledge and experience in international affairs and at least ten years at the Bahamas Bar;

(e) a certified public accountant registered under The Bahamas Institute and Chartered Accountants Act (Ch. 364); and

(f) two other persons with knowledge and experience in international affairs.

(5) The members of the Foreign Service Committee shall be appointed for a term of three years and may be reappointed for a further term of three years.

(6) The Minister shall appoint the chairperson from among the members of the Foreign Service Committee.

The Director General shall be the Chief Advisor to the Foreign Service Committee.

The members of the Foreign Service Committee shall be paid a fee as approved by the Minister.

Subject to this Act and the Foreign Service Orders, the Foreign Service Committee shall determine its own rules and procedures.

**9.** Foreign Service Appeal Tribunal.

(1) There is established for the purposes of this Act, a body to be known as the Foreign Service Appeal Tribunal.

(2) The Foreign Service Appeal Tribunal shall hear appeals from employees aggrieved by decisions of the Foreign Service Committee under section 8(3).

(3) The Foreign Service Appeal Tribunal may affirm or set aside the decision appealed from the Foreign Service Committee or may make any other decision.

(4) The members of the Foreign Service Appeal Tribunal shall be appointed by the Minister for a period not exceeding three years.

(5) The members of the Foreign Service Appeal Tribunal shall consist of —

(a) a Justice or retired Justice of the Supreme Court or Court of Appeal who shall be the Chairperson;

(b) a retired Permanent Secretary; and

(c) a person with knowledge and experience in the foreign service. (6) The members of the Foreign Service Appeal Tribunal shall be paid a fee as approved by the Minister.

(7) Subject to this Act and the Foreign Service Orders, the Foreign Service Appeal Tribunal shall determine its own rules and procedures.

**10.** Transfer of public officers.

(1) All public officers and contract officers assigned to the Department at the commencement of this Act, shall be transferred with their consent from the service of the Government to the service of the Department. (2) Every public officer and contract officer who has transferred to the Department under subsection (1) shall be employed at a salary and on terms and conditions no less favourable than those which were attached to his appointment with the Government at the time of transfer to the Department.

(3) Every public officer and contract officer transferred to the Department shall be deemed to have ceased to be in the service of the Government on the date of transfer to the Department.

Pensions of persons transferred from public service to the

Where a public officer ceases to be the holder of an office in the public service by reason of his transfer to the service of the Department, and that person subsequently retires from the service of the Department in such circumstances that, had he remained a public officer he would have been eligible for pension under the Pensions Act (Ch. 43), then in that case subsections (2) and (3) shall have effect.

(2) A pension payable to a person mentioned in subsection (1) by the Department, shall be calculated and granted to him in respect of his service in accordance with section 12(3).

(3) There shall be payable out of the Consolidated Fund to the Department, upon the warrant of the Minister of Finance, such contributions towards the grant of pensions as prescribed in section 12.

**12.** Pension for Staff of the Department.

(1) The Department shall –

(a) establish and maintain a contributory pension plan to which a person employed after the commencement of this Act and the Department, are responsible for making contributions (the

“Contributions Plan”);

(b) establish and maintain a non-contributory pension plan for public officers who held pensionable offices under the Government of The Bahamas and were transferred in accordance with section 9, from the public service to the Department (the “Benefits Plan”).

(2) There shall be payable out of the Consolidated Fund to the Benefits Plan in respect of the subsequent retirement from the Department of an employee referred to in subsection (1)(b), a contribution of such amount as would have been payable to such employee by way of pension under section 22 of the Pensions Act (Ch.43) as if that person had retired from the public service in such circumstances that, had he remained a public officer, he would have been eligible for pension under the Pensions Act (Ch.43).

(3) In calculating the pension contribution payable under subsection (2), the employee's service under the Government shall be deemed to be continuous with his service with the Department, that is, his total service with the Government and the Department.

(4) A contribution to the Contributions Plan established pursuant to subsection (1)(a) shall be paid out of the Consolidated Fund to the Department in respect of a person employed by the Department, who was

not transferred from a public office, and who elects to join the Contributions Plan.

The Minister after consultation with the Director General shall by regulations provide for the establishment of the Contributions Plan under subsection (1)(a), the rules of eligibility, the required contributions, the amount of pension, and other matters with respect to the grant of a pension.

(6) Every public officer who held a pensionable office under the Government of The Bahamas before the commencement of this Act and who transferred to the Department shall continue to receive a pension under the Pensions Act (Ch.43) until the Benefits Plan is established under subsection (1)(b).

**13.** Bahamas Alrae Ramsey Institute of Foreign Affairs.

*be*

There is established for the purposes of this Act, a diplomatic training centre known as The Bahamas Alrae Ramsey Institute of Foreign Affairs, which shall responsible for the training in diplomacy of all foreign service officers in the Department.

**14.** Foreign Service Orders.

*The*

Minister may, after consultation with the Foreign Service Committee, make Orders —

(a) to govern and regulate the foreign service of The Bahamas and the conduct, classification, ranks, and terms and conditions of service of —

(i) foreign service officers; and

(ii) foreign service employees, including non-Bahamian staff locally employed at a mission; and

(b) providing for such other matters as may be required in order for the Department to effectively and efficiently perform the functions of the Department.

**15.** Regulations.

*The*

Minister may, after consultation with the Foreign Service Committee, make regulations —

(a) establishing, codifying and regulating the issuance and terms and conditions of visas for the entry of persons who are not citizens or permanent residents of The Bahamas; and

(b) imposing fees to be charged for consular services including fees for the physical delivery of passports visas and other documents by

courier services to persons making application for consular services.

Savings and Transitional.

(1) Every document granted or issued under the Ministry of Foreign Affairs Act in force immediately before the coming into force of this Act continues in force after the commencement of this Act, on the same conditions and with the same effect as if this Act had not come into force. (2) An appointment made under the Ministry of Foreign Affairs Act, before the coming into force of this Act continues and is valid until that appointment is revoked, amended or another appointment is made under this Act.

(3) On the date of commencement of this Act —

(a) the department operating within the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and known as the Department of Foreign Affairs, is replaced by the Department established under this Act;

(b) all legal obligations and liabilities of the Department of Foreign Affairs shall be the obligations and liabilities of the Department.

(c) the person who holds the post of Director General shall continue in that post as if the person was appointed under this Act; and

(d) every foreign service officer or foreign service employee who held a post in the Department of of Foreign Affairs shall continue in that post as if it was granted under this Act.

*The*

POWERS, DUTIES AND FUNCTIONS OF THE DIRECTOR GENERAL duties of the Director General are to —

(a) conduct all diplomatic and consular relations on behalf of The Bahamas; (b) be a technical advisor to the Minister on foreign policy issues;

(c) be responsible for the technical direction of the Department;

(d) provide briefings to the public on foreign service matters and policy issues relating to the foreign service as directed by the Minister;

(e) prepare analytical assessment and forecasting of issues affecting The Bahamas;

(f) monitor development of bilateral relations and provide policy guidance; (g) monitor relations with multilateral organizations and institutions and provide policy guidance;

(h) coordinate the directions given by the Government of The Bahamas to the heads of The Bahamas' diplomatic and consular missions on foreign policy issues;

manage The Bahamas' diplomatic and consular missions;

(j) administer the foreign service of The Bahamas;

(k) manage and organize professional development and training of foreign service officers;

(l) coordinate foreign policy issues with the respective Departments in the Public Service;

(m) prepare reports and obligations under the International Treaties and Conventions;

(n) coordinate foreign policy briefs on issues relevant to The Bahamas; (o) coordinate information on the Ministry’s website and social media; (p) represent The Bahamas at bilateral and and multilateral meetings; (q) coordinate diplomatic representation;

(r) coordinate the functions of the Protocol Department;

(s) provide periodic briefing to the departments in the Public Service on foreign affairs and policy issues;

(t) provide briefing on the role of the Department to agencies of the Government;

(u) produce annual reports of the Department;

(v) prepare Cabinet Memoranda;

(w) maintain a Treaty Register of all bilateral and multilateral treaties and conventions applicable to The Bahamas; and

(x) carry out any other duties and function that are by law assigned to the Director General.
