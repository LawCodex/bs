# Hotel (1924) Act

*IV – 36]*

Act No. 2 of 1924

### HOTEL (1924)

*2 of 1924 8 of 1927 26 of 1928*

An Act to provide hotel accommodation in the Eastern District of New Providence.

[Commencement 24th April, 1924]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Hotel (1924) Act.

*Governor in Council authorised to loan £ 150,000 for erection of hotel.*

**2.** (1) It shall be lawful for the Governor in Council to loan a sum, not exceeding one hundred and fifty thousand pounds, for the erection of a fireproof hotel consisting of approximately 200 guest rooms and other necessary accommodation on the site known as “Waterloo” in the Eastern District of New Providence, or on any other suitable site in New Providence.

*26 of 1928. s. 2.*

(2) The said sum of one hundred and fifty thousand pounds shall be loaned on terms and conditions similar as far as possible to those contained in the Agreement dated the twenty-fourth day of November, 1922, and set out in the Schedule to the Hotel and Steamship Service Act 1923, 3 save that the rate of interest to be paid on the sum loaned shall be at the rate of four pounds per centum per annum and no interest shall commence to accrue until the first day of January, 1931.

*Loan payable out of Treasury.*

**3.** The said sum of one hundred and fifty thousand pounds or such part thereof as may be loaned under the provisions of section 2 of this Act, shall be paid out of the Treasury by warrant in the usual manner.

*Governor in Council may make further advance not exceeding £70,000.*

**4.** (1) In addition to the sum which may be loaned under the foregoing provisions of this Act it shall be lawful for the Governor in Council to loan a further sum not exceeding seventy thousand pounds to the same Company and for the same purpose.

*8 of 1927, s. 2.*
