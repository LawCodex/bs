# Village Estates, Limited, Hotel Act

*IV – 190]*

### VILLAGE ESTATES, LIMITED, HOTEL

VILLAGE ESTATES, LIMITED, HOTEL Act No. 70 of 1957

*70 of 1957*

VILLAGE ESTATES, LIMITED, HOTEL An Act to authorise the entering into of an agreement with Village Estates, Limited, in respect of an hotel in Soldier Road in the Eastern District of the Island of New Providence.

[Commencement 22nd July, 1957]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Village Estates, Limited, Hotel Act.

*Authorises Governor in Council to enter into Agreement.*

*Ch. 289.*

**2.** The Governor in Council is hereby authorised to enter into an Agreement with Village Estates, Limited, in respect of the construction of an Hotel on the Eastern side of Soldier Road in the Eastern District of the Island of New Providence as if it were a new hotel within the meaning of the Hotels Encouragement Act.
