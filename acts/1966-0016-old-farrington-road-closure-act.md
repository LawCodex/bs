# Old Farrington Road Closure Act

*IV – 270]*

### OLD FARRINGTON ROAD CLOSURE

OLD FARRINGTON ROAD CLOSURE Act No. 16 of 1966

*16 of 1966*

OLD FARRINGTON ROAD CLOSURE An Act to authorize the stopping up of the Old Farrington Road in the Western District of New Providence.

[Commencement 27th May, 1966] WHEREAS the course of Farrington Road in the Western District of the Island of New Providence in the vicinity of the sub-division known as Cunningham Acres has been diverted and certain portions of the existing public road have become disused:

and WHEREAS certain owners and occupiers of proper-ties adjacent to the existing public road have made proposals for the purchase of the said portions:

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Old Farrington Road Closure Act 1966.

*Authorises road closure and conveyances.*

**2.** Notwithstanding the provisions of any other Act, it shall be lawful —

(a) for the Treasurer to convey in fee simple to any owner or occupier of land adjacent thereto those portions of the old Farrington Road which are delineated on a plan filed with a copy of this Act in the Department of Public Works, in consideration of such sums of money or other consideration as the Minister for Works may deem good and sufficient; and

(b) for the Minister for Works to stop up and close those portions of the old Farrington Road conveyed by the Treasurer under the provisions of this section.

*Cesser of public right-of-way.*

**3.** Upon the said portions of the public road being stopped up and closed in manner provided by paragraph (b) of section 2 of this Act, the right of the public to the use of the same shall cease and be extinguished.
