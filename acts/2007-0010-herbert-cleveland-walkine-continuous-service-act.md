# Herbert Cleveland Walkine (Continuous Service) Act

*[IV – 385*

### HERBERT CLEVELAND WALKINE (CONTINUOUS SERVICE)

Act No. 10 of 2007

### HERBERT CLEVELAND WALKINE (CONTINUOUS SERVICE) ACT

An Act to provide for the continuous service in the public service of Herbert Cleveland Walkine

[Assent 20th March, 2007]

[Commencement 21st March, 2007]

*Short title.*

*Ch. 43. Computation of pension period. Ch. 43.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Herbert Cleveland Walkine (Continuous Service) Act and shall be read and construed as one with the Pensions Act.

**2.** Notwithstanding subsection (1) of section 24 of the Pensions Act, in computing the period of service in respect of which Herbert Cleveland Walkine may be granted a pension or gratuity, the period 1952 to August 1958 shall be taken into account and deemed continuous with the period that he served in the public service.
