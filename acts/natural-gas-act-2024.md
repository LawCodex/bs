*b c d*

to establish a legislative framework to regulate the importation, regasification, shipping, transportation, and retailing of natural gas to ensure these activities are performed in a safe efficient effective economic and environmental manner to promote access to gas in an affordable and safe manner to provide higher fuel efficiency to lower costs for consumers to promote the global effort in reducing emissions that contribute to global climate change, thus serving to sustain the environment for future generations.
