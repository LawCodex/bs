# Hog Island (Change of Name) Act

*[IV – 235*

### HOG ISLAND (CHANGE OF NAME)

### HOG ISLAND (CHANGE OF NAME)

Act No. 22 of 1962 HOG ISLAND (CHANGE OF NAME)

*22 of 1962*

An Act to change to Paradise Island the name of the island lying to the north of the Island of New Providence and known as Hog Island.

[Commencement 23rd May, 1962]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Hog Island (Change of Name) Act.

*Renaming of Island.*

**2.** From and after the coming into force of this Act the Island lying to the North of the Island of New Providence heretofore known as Hog Island shall be called and referred to as Paradise Island.

*Reference to Hog Island to mean Paradise Island.*

**3.** Wheresoever in any Act, Order in Council, regulation or rule passed or official notice published before the coming into force of this Act reference is made to Hog Island the same shall, unless the context otherwise requires, be construed to mean Paradise Island.
