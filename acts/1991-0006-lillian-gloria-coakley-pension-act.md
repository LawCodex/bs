# Lillian Gloria Coakley Pension Act

*[IV – 361*

### LILLIAN GLORIA COAKLEY PENSION

LILLIAN GLORIA COAKLEY PENSION Act No. 6 of 1991

### LILLIAN GLORIA COAKLEY PENSION

## ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS

### SECTION

**1.** Short title and commencement.

**2.** Pension.

**3.** Charge on Consolidated Fund.

**4.** Recovery of advances

*6 of 1991*

An Act to provide a pension for Lillian Gloria Coakley.

[Assent 9th July, 1991]

[Commencement 10th February, 1985]

*Short title and commencement.*

**1.** (1) This Act may be cited as the Lillian Gloria Coakley Pension Act, 1991.

(2) This Act shall be deemed to have come into force on the 10th day of February, 1985.

*Pension.*

**2.** Notwithstanding anything to the contrary con-tained in any other Act relating to the grant of pensions and gratuities, a pension is granted to Lillian Gloria Coakley at the rate of eight thousand five hundred dollars per annum.

*Charge on Consolidated Fund.*

**3.** There is charged on and paid out of the Consolidated Fund all such sums of money as may be granted by way of pension in accordance with this Act.

*Recovery of advances*

**4.** The Treasurer may recover from any payment of pension under this Act the amount of any advances made to Lillian Gloria Coakley before the commencement of this Act on account of or in respect of any claim to pension.
