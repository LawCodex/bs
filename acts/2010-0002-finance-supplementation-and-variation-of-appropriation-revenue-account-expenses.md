**1.** Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Finance (Supplementation and Variation of Appropriation) (Revenue Account Expenses) (2009) Act, 2010.

**2.** Construction of Act No.16 of 2009.

In this Act and in any other written law, a reference to the Appropriation (Revenue Account Expenses) (2009/2010) Act, 2009 ("the Appropriation Act, 2009") shall be construed as a reference to that Act as for the time being varied or otherwise amended by this Act.

**3.** Increase In Head of Expenditure.

The sums appropriated to the Head of Expenditure set out in the First Column of

Part I of the Schedule to this Act l y the Appropriation Act, 2009 are increased by the amounts specified in the Sec· nd Column of that Part.

## Part II of the Schedule to this Act by the Appropriation Act, 2009 are reduced by the amounts specified in the Second Column of that Part.
