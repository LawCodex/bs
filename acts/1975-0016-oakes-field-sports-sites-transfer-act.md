# Oakes Field Sports Sites (Transfer) Act

*IV – 346]*

Act No. 16 of 1975

*16 of 1975*

OAKES FIELD SPORTS SITES (TRANSFER) An Act to transfer to the Treasurer certain lands vested in the Minister for Sport.

[Commencement 12th September, 1975]

*16 of 1975*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Oakes Field Sports Sites (Transfer) Act.

*Short title.*

**2.** With effect from the date of commencement of this Act and without further assurance, the lands described in the Schedule and hitherto vested in the Minister for Sport by virtue of section 3(2) of the Sports Act, are transferred to the Treasurer in fee simple of all trusts and encumbrances.

## SCHEDULE

**1.** ALL THAT piece of land destined as a site for a secondary school measuring 16 acres or thereabouts being part of a parcel of Government land set aside as a sports area at Oakes field and situate northwardly of Yellow Elder Gardens Subdivision Phase II and approximately three quarters of a mile westwardly of the Harrold Road Blue Hill Road Roundabout in the Western District of the Island of New Providence in the Commonwealth of The Bahamas which said piece of land is shown on the plan recorded in the Department of Lands and Surveys as Plan No. 636 of New Providence ABUTTING AND BOUNDING towards the WEST NORTH and EAST on other lands of Government set aside as a sports area and towards the SOUTH on a reservation for a road 100 feet wide leading to Harrold Road or however else the same may abut and bound which said piece of land is more particularly described and has such position shape size boundaries and dimensions as are set out on a copy of the said plan No. 636 attached hereto and marked or distinguished by pink lines and marked “A”.
