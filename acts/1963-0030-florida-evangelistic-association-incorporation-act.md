# Florida Evangelistic Association Incorporation Act

*[IV – 243*

INCORPORATION Act No. 30 of 1963

*30 of 1963*

FLORIDA EVANGELISTIC ASSOCIATION INCORPORATION An Act to incorporate the Field Superintendent of the Florida Evangelistic Association.

[Commencement 31st July, 1963]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Florida Evangelistic Association Incorporation Act.

*Field Superintendent of the Florida Evangelistic Association to be a corporation sole. Power of Field Superintendent of the Florida Evangelistic Association to acquire property.*

**2.** The Field Superintendent of the Florida Evangelistic Association for the time being shall be deemed a corporation sole and shall be known and described as

“Field Superintendent of the Florida Evangelistic Association”.

**3.** (1) The Field Superintendent of the Florida Evangelistic Association shall have full power to acquire by purchase, transfer, donation, exchange, devise, bequest, grant, gift, conveyance or otherwise any real or personal property or any estate or interest therein.

(2) All real or personal property acquired under subsection (1) of this section shall be held for the use and benefit of the Florida Evangelistic Association.

*Field Superintendent of the Florida Evangelistic Association entitled to deal with property.*

**4.** The Field Superintendent of the Florida Evangelistic Association shall, subject to any restraint, reservation or condition contained in the document under which he shall have acquired title thereto be entitled to sell, mortgage, lease and otherwise dispose of and deal with all property which may for the time being be vested in or have been acquired by him under any of the provisions of this Act.

*Change of person holding office of Field Superintendent not to affect property and interest.*

**5.** No change of the person holding the office of Field Superintendent of the Florida Evangelistic Association shall affect any real or personal property or any estate or interest therein vested in the Field Superintendent of the Florida Evangelistic Association but such property and estate and interest therein shall vest in the succeeding Field Superintendent of the Florida Evangelistic Association in

*Power of Field Superintendent of the Florida Evangelistic Association to appoint an Attorney. Present Field Superintendent of the Florida Evangelistic Association.*

**6.** The Field Superintendent of the Florida Evangelistic Association shall have power to appoint an Attorney for such time and purposes and with such powers as may be stated in the Power of Attorney.

**7.** The Reverend Clarence J. Goodspeed shall be deemed to be the present Field Superintendent of the Florida Evangelistic Association.

*Vesting property at present held in trust for the Florida Evangelistic Association.*

**8.** All property in The Bahamas real or personal of what nature or kind soever which is now vested or held in any manner in trust for or for the use and benefit of the Florida Evangelistic Association shall vest in and be held by the Field Superintendent of the Florida Evangelistic Association.

*Rights of the Queen and others reserved.*

**9.** Nothing in this Act shall prejudice or affect the rights of Her Majesty the Queen, Her Heirs and Successors or of any other person or persons except such as are mentioned in this Act and those claiming by, from, through or under them.

*Commencement.*

**10.** This Act shall not come into operation unless and until the Governor notifies by proclamation that it is Her Majesty’s pleasure not to disallow the same.
