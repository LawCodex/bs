## PART II - NATIONAL YOUTH COMMISSION

Establishment of the Commission.

(1) There is hereby established a body to be known as the National Youth Commission.

(2) The Commission shall be a body corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal and may —

(a) acquire, hold and dispose of any real and personal property;

(b) sue and be sued in its corporate name;

(c) enter into contracts;

(d) establish and operate bank accounts; and

(e) subject to this Act, perform all acts that bodies corporate may by law perform.

(3) The governing body of the Commission shall be the Board of Directors. (4) The provisions of the Schedule shall have effect with respect to the constitution and procedures of the Board of Directors and otherwise in relation thereto.

**4.** Committees of Board.

(1) The Board may for the discharge of its functions appoint one or more committees to perform any function of the Board as the Board may determine.

(2) A committee shall submit the report of its proceedings to the Board at a time to be determined by the Board.

### III – POWERS, DUTIES AND FUNCTIONS OF THE COMMISSION

**5.** Powers duties and functions of Commission.

(1) The Commission shall implement policies, programmes and projects consistent with the National Youth Policy which will empower the youth to develop their potential for national development.

(2) Without prejudice to the generality of subsection (1) the Commission shall —

(a) assist in the creation of employment opportunities for the youth and develop medium and long-term strategies to reduce youth unemployment in The Bahamas;

(b) initiate youth development programmes in collaboration with relevant governmental and non-governmental bodies;

coordinate and monitor the youth development programmes; (d) revise the National Youth Policy every three years;

(e) collaborate with other governmental or non-governmental bodies in the provision of youth skills training programmes and schemes; (f) provide a focal point for addressing drug abuse by the youth and its related problems;

(g) create a reliable and efficient network by which the youth may access valuable information on beneficial services and intensive-driven amenities provided by governmental and non-governmental bodies;

(h) coordinate the activities of youth groups and youth-servicing organisations;

(i) register youth groups and youth-servicing organisations; and (j) register youth workers and issue certificates of registration; (k) investigate youth organizations and youth workers not acting in interest of youth; and

(l) carry out any other function that contributes to the implementation of the National Youth Policy.

## PART V – FINANCIAL PROVISIONS

Funds of the Commission.

(1) The funds of the Commission shall consist of —

(a) sums provided by Parliament for the functions of the Commission;

(b) all fees payable to the Commission;

(c) gifts, donations, sponsorships, endowments, property and other contributions lawfully received by the Commission whether local or international;

(d) income derived from services or other lawful activities provided by the Commission.

(2) The funds of the Commission may be expended only —

(a) in payment or discharge of expenses, obligations and liabilities of the Commission;

(b) in payment of the remuneration and allowances of members of the Commission and of employees of the Commission;

(c) for such other purposes that are consistent with the functions of the Commission as the Board, after consultation with the Minister may determine.

(3) The financial operations of the Commission shall be governed in accordance with the Public Finance Management Act.

**12.** Accounts and audit.

(1) The Commission shall keep proper records in relation to the accounts, and shall prepare in respect of each financial year, a statement of accounts. (2) The accounts of the Commission shall each year be audited by the Auditor-General or an auditor appointed by the Board with the approval of the Minister from among members of The Bahamas Institute of Chartered Accountants.

(3) The Minister shall cause a copy of the annual financial statement of accounts together with the auditor's report to be laid before both chambers of Parliament and shall be published in the Gazette.

**13.** Budget estimates and annual report.

(1) The Commission shall prepare for each financial year an annual budget of revenue and expenditure.

The annual budget referred to under subsection (1), shall be submitted to the Minister four months before the beginning of the financial year.

The Commission shall, three months after the end of each financial year, submit to the Minister a report of its activities, accompanied by a statement of its expenditure and revenue.

(4) The Minister shall cause a copy of the report of the Commission to be laid before both chambers of Parliament.

### VI – REGISTRATION OF YOUTH ORGANISATIONS

**14.** Register of youth organisations.

(1) The Commission shall keep and maintain a register called the National Youth Organisation Register in which shall be recorded the names of all registered youth organisations and other information as determined by the Commission.

(2) The register shall be made available to the public.

**15.** Registration of youth organisations.

(1) An organization providing services and support to or for the youth shall not carry out operations unless it is registered with the Commission and shall renew the registration annually.

(2) An organization shall submit to the Commission, an application to register in accordance with the prescribed form and manner.

(3) Any organization of youth and for youth in existence immediately before the commencement of this Act must be registered within three years from the date of commencement of this Act.

(4) A person who carries on operations without registering in accordance with this section, or continues operation while registration has been suspended or revoked commits an offence and is liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding ten thousand dollars or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding one year, or to both.

**16.** Requirements for registration of youth organisations.

*An by*

organisation providing youth-centred services and support shall be registered the Commission, if the Commission is satisfied that the organisation —

(a) has an organisational plan with a mission, vision and curriculum;

(b) has a safeguarding policy specific to that organisation;

(c) provides evidence that their meeting space is safe and meets physical and environmental health standards;

(d) provides evidence of the appropriate youth leader to youth ratio;

(e) has all executives and youth workers registered by the Commission in the National Youth Worker Register; and

(f) has paid the prescribed fee.

**17.** Registration certificate.

(1) Where the Commission is satisfied that an organisation has met the requirements for registration, the Commission shall approve its application and shall issue a certificate of registration to the organisation upon payment of the prescribed fee.

(2) A certificate of registration issued under subsection (1) is valid for a period of one year from the date of issue.

**18.** Renewal of registration.

(1) An organisation shall make application for renewal of its registration in the prescribed form and upon payment of the prescribed fee for renewal annually.

(2) Where the Commission is satisfied that the organisation that makes an application under subsection (1) continues to meet the requirements for registration under section 16, the Commission shall issue a certificate of registration to the organisation

**19.** Cancellation or suspension of registration of an organization.

(1) The Commission shall cancel or suspend the registration of a youth organisation where the organisation —

(a) engages a person with an adverse criminal record or history of offences against children or youth;

(b) is complacent in breaches of safeguarding policies;

(c) fails to disclose safeguarding incidents to the Commission;

(d) allows harm to a youth while in the care of the organization;

(e) gives false information to the Commission;

(f) operates in a manner that is not in accordance with this Act, any Regulations made under this Act or the National Youth Policy; or

(g) ceases to meet requirements under section 16.

(2) Where the Commission determines that an investigation into the operations of the youth organisation is required, the Commission may suspend the registration of the organisation during the period of investigation.

Where the registration of the youth organisation is liable to be cancelled or suspended the Commission shall give notice to the organisation in writing —

(a) that its registration is liable to be cancelled or suspended at the end of a period determined by the Commission being not less than thirty days from the date of the notice; and

(b) stating the reasons why the youth organisation is liable to be cancelled or suspended.

(4) Before exercising its power to cancel or suspend the registration of an organisation under this section, the Commission shall give the organisation an opportunity to show cause, in writing, why its registration should not be cancelled or suspended.

(5) Where the registration of a youth organisation is cancelled under this section its name shall be removed from the register.

(6) Where the registration of a youth organisation is cancelled or suspended under this section, the Commission shall cause a notice of cancellation or suspension to be published in the Gazette, a local daily newspaper and an official Government website.

**20.** Reinstatement of registration

(1) Where an organisation that has been cancelled or suspended from the register desires to be reinstated to the register, it shall apply to the Commission to be reinstated in the prescribed form.

(2) The Commission may, with or without conditions, reinstate a youth organisation to the register if it appears to the Commission that there has been a change in circumstances which justify the reinstatement.

**21.** Right of appeal.

*An to —*

organisation may appeal to the court against a decision of the Commission

(a) refuse an application for registration; or

(b) cancel or suspend its registration.

## PART VII – YOUTH WORKERS

**22.** Register of youth workers.

(1) The Commission shall keep and maintain a register called the National Youth Worker Register in which shall be recorded the names of all registered youth workers.

The register shall be made available to the public.

Registration of youth workers.

Every person who carries out activities or services to, for or with the youth shall register as a youth worker with the Commission.

(2) The form and manner of registration shall be as prescribed.

(3) Every registration approved by the Commission shall —

(a) be recorded in the register of youth workers;

(b) be valid from the date of registration;

(c) expire on the 31 st day of December in each year; and

(d) be renewable annually.

(4) Where an application for registration is refused, the reasons for the refusal shall be given to the applicant in writing.

**24.** Requirements for registration as a youth worker.

*A the*

person shall be registered as a youth worker if the Commission is satisfied that person —

(a) is eighteen years old or older;

(b) has no criminal convictions;

(c) has obtained qualification and training in youth work; and

(d) has paid the prescribed registration fee.

**25.** Qualification in youth work.

*For for*

the purposes of section 22, a person satisfies the qualification requirements registration as a youth worker if the person holds —

(a) a Youth Leaders Certification from the Ministry of Youth; or

(b) a diploma, certificate or degree in a course approved by the Commission from an institution approved by the Commission.

**26.** Renewal of Registration.

(1) Every registered youth worker shall renew his registration annually not later than the 31 st day of January in each year.

(2) The Commission shall renew the registration of a youth worker if it is satisfied that the youth worker —

(a) continues to meet the requirements for registration referred to under section 22;

(b) has completed a minimum of twenty hours of continuing professional development, ten of which shall be structured and ten of which shall be flexible; and

(c) has paid the required renewal fee.

Continuing professional development.

Every calendar year, a youth worker shall undertake —

(a) a minimum of twenty hours of continuing professional development, ten of which shall be structured and ten of which shall be flexible, or as prescribed; and

(b) safeguarding training by a local institution approved by the Commission.

(2) For the purpose of subsection (1) —

(a) structured continuing professional development takes the form of workshops, diploma or certification courses approved by the Commission; and

(b) flexible continuing professional development takes the form of conferences, peer learning, reflective journals and policy forums.

**28.** Cancellation or suspension of registration of a youth worker. (1) The Commission shall suspend the registration of a registered youth worker where the youth worker is charged with a crime or under investigation by the Commission.

(2) The Commission shall cancel the registration of a registered youth worker where the youth worker —

(a) is convicted of a crime;

(b) failed to report an incident of harm or abuse toward a youth;

(c) ceased to maintain professional standards;

(d) engages in any conduct that harms a youth or puts a youth at risk;

(e) misuse of programs funds; or

(f) gives false information to the Commission.

**29.** Investigation by the Commission.

(1) The Commission may, either on its own motion or on a complaint by any person, institute an investigation into the conduct of a youth worker registered under this Act for an alleged act or omission in contravention of this Act or regulations made under this Act.

(2) Where the Commission determines that an investigation into the conduct of a youth worker is required, the Commission may suspend the registration of the youth worker during the period of investigation. (3) Where the registration of the youth worker is liable to be cancelled or suspended the Commission shall give notice to the youth worker in writing stating the reasons why the youth organisation is liable to be cancelled or suspended.

Before exercising its power to cancel or suspend the registration of a youth worker under this section, the Commission shall give the youth worker an opportunity to show cause, in writing, why its registration should not be cancelled or suspended.

(5) Where the registration of a youth worker is cancelled under this section the name of the youth worker shall be removed from the register. (6) Where the registration of a youth worker is cancelled or suspended under this section, the Commission shall cause a notice of cancellation or suspension to be published in the Gazette, a local daily newspaper and an official Government website.

**30.** Reinstatement of registration of a youth worker.

(1) Where the registration of a youth worker has been cancelled or suspended desires to be reinstated to the register, the youth worker shall apply to the Commission to be reinstated in the prescribed form.

(2) The Commission may, with or without conditions, reinstate the youth worker to the register if it appears to the Commission that there has been a change in circumstances which justify the reinstatement.

**31.** Right of appeal.

*A*

person may appeal to the court against a decision of the Commission to —

(a) refuse an application for registration; or

(b) cancel or suspend the person's registration.

## PART VIII - MISCELLANEOUS

**32.** Establishment of youth advisory committees.

(1) Every District Council established under section 18 of the Local Government Act, 2024 (No. 59 of 2024) in the Family Islands shall establish a youth advisory committee.

(2) A youth advisory committee shall —

(a) assist the Commission in the performance of its functions in that locality;

(b) sensitise the youth in the locality on the objectives of the Commission and mobilise them to achieve the objectives;

(c) submit recommendations to the Commission on the measures to be adopted for the achievement of the object of the Commission.

Role of the Minister.

Without prejudice to other provisions of this Act, the Minister shall —

(a) oversee the proper implementation of the Act; and

(b) review and approve the National Youth Policy and revision of it.

**34.** Regulations.

The Minister may make regulations generally for the better carrying out of the provisions of this Act and without prejudice to the generality of the power thereby conferred, may make regulations, providing for —

(a) the time, place and manner when, in which and how the Commission shall exercise its functions;

(b) the terms and conditions of appointment to any office in the service of the Commission; and

(c) the preservation of all property vested in the Commission and the proper and economic use of all such property.

Constitution and Procedures of the Board of Directors of the Commission

**1.** Constitution of the Board of Directors.

There shall be a Board of Directors of the Commission which shall consist of —

(a) a Chairman;

(b) three persons from civil society;

(c) three persons representing Youth groups, one of whom shall be from a Family Island;

(d) the Youth Commissioner who shall be secretary to the Board;

(e) the Director of Youth;

(f) the Financial Secretary or his designee; and

(g) the Permanent Secretary of the Ministry responsible for Youth affairs.

**2.** Common seal of the Commission.

The Commission shall have a common seal the use of which shall be authenticated by the signature of the Commissioner and any other member of the Board designated for that purpose by the Board.

**3.** Tenure of members of the Board.

(1) A member of the Board who is not a member by virtue of an office, shall be appointed for a period of three years, and shall be eligible for reappointment.

(2) A member of the Board who is a member by virtue of his office shall continue to be a member of the Board as long as he holds that office.

**4.** Revocation of appointment.

The Minister may revoke the appointment of any appointed member, if satisfied that the member —

(a) has been absent from three consecutive meetings of the Board without the permission of the Board;

(b) has been adjudged or otherwise declared bankrupt under any law in force in The Bahamas;

(c) is incapacitated by physical or mental illness;

(d) is sentenced to a term of imprisonment; or

(e) is otherwise unable or unfit to discharge the functions of a member of the Board.

Leave of absence and resignation.

The Minister may on the application of any member grant to each member leave of absence for any period not exceeding six months.

(2) If any member other than the Chairman is granted leave of absence in accordance with the provisions of subparagraph (1) or has been appointed to act as Chairman, the Minister may appoint a suitable person to act temporarily in place of that member.

(3) The Chairman may at any time resign his office by instrument in writing addressed to the Minister and such resignation shall take effect as from the date of the receipt by the Minister of such instrument.

(4) Any member of the Commission other than the Chairman may at any time resign his office by instrument in writing addressed to the Minister and transmitted through the Chairman and, upon the date of the receipt, by the Chairman of each instrument, such member shall cease to be a member of the Commission.

(5) The appointment, revocation of appointment, resignation or death of any member of the Commission shall be notified in the Gazette.

**6.** Filling of vacancies.

(1) Where the Chairman or other member of the Board dies, resigns, is removed from office or is absent from The Bahamas for a continuous period exceeding three months or is by reason of illness unable to perform the functions of his office —

(a) the other members of the Board shall elect one of their number to act as Chairman until such time as the Chairman resumes his office or another is appointed in his stead; or

(b) in the case of any other member other than the Chairman, the Chairman shall arrange, subject to the Act, to have another person appointed to the Board.

(2) Where a person is elected Chairman or appointed as a member to fill a vacancy, he or she shall hold office for the remainder of the term of the previous Chairman or other member, as the case may be, and shall, subject to this Act be eligible for re-appointment.

**7.** Remuneration of Members of the Board.

*The an*

Chairman and the members of the Board who are not members by virtue of office shall be entitled to the remuneration, fees or allowances as may be determined by the Minister.

**8.** Meeting of Board.

The Board shall meet for the transaction of business at a place and time as the Chairman may decide but shall meet at least once every three months.

The Chairman shall, at the request in writing of not less than four other members of the Commission, call an extraordinary meeting of the Board at a place and time the Chairman may determine.

(3) The Chairman shall preside at the meetings of the Board and in his absence the members present shall elect one of their number to preside. (4) The Board may at any time co-opt any person to act as an adviser at its meeting but the co-opted person shall not be entitled to vote on any matter for decision by the Board.

(5) A member of the Board who has any interest, whether direct or indirect, in any matter being considered by the Board, shall disclose the nature of his or her interest to the Board and the disclosure shall be recorded in the minutes of the Board and the member shall not take part in any deliberation or decision of the Board relating to that matter; and a member who contravenes this subsection may be removed from the Board for misconduct.

(6) The quorum of a meeting of the Board shall be six.

(7) The decisions of the Board shall be made by a majority of the votes of the members present and where the votes are equal, the Chairman or other person presiding shall have a casting vote.

(8) Any proposal circulated among all members of the Board and agreed to in writing by a two-thirds majority of all the members shall be of the same force and effect as a decision made at a duly constituted meeting of the Board and shall be incorporated in the minutes of the next meeting of the Board; but if a member of the Board requires that the proposal be placed before a meeting of the Board, this decision shall not apply to the proposal.

(9) Subject to this paragraph, the Board shall regulate the procedure at its meetings.
