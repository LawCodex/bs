# International Properties Limited (Validation of Conveyance) Act

### OF CONVEYANCE)

Act No. 22 of 1966

### INTERNATIONAL PROPERTIES LIMITED (VALIDATION OF CONVEYANCE)

*22 of 1966*

An Act to validate and confirm a conveyance to international properties limited of certain lands situate at Delaport Point in the Western District of the Island of New Providence, one of The Bahama Islands.

[Commencement 12th July, 1966]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the International Proper-ties Limited (Validation of Conveyance) Act.

*Definition.*

**2.** In this Act the expression “the Conveyance” means the conveyance short particulars of which are set out in the First Schedule hereto; “the property” means the property described in the Second Schedule hereto; and “the Company” means International Properties Limited, a Company incorporated under the laws of the Bahama Islands, which has been struck off the Register of Companies under The Removal of Defunct Companies Act.

*Validates the Conveyance.*

*Ch. 308.*

**3.** For the avoidance of doubts, it is hereby declared that, notwithstanding the consideration stated in the Conveyance, or the provisions of the Companies Act, or any other matters or objections:

(a) The Conveyance shall for all purposes be deemed to be and always to have been good, valid and effective; and

*Validates title of Purchaser under the Conveyance.*

(b) The property shall for all purposes (whether before or after the passing of this Act) be deemed to have been conveyed to the Company, the Purchaser named in the Conveyance, for an estate in unincumbered fee simple in possession on the date of the Conveyance and accordingly the title of the Company to the said property by virtue of the Conveyance is hereby confirmed and validated.

*IV – 272]*

*Extinguishes claims.*

*Ch. 308.*

**4.** All claims (if any) to the property adverse to the Company or to its successors in title that may heretofore have arisen or become vested in any person or persons (whether the Treasurer of The Bahamas or any other person) as a result of any lack or inadequacy of consideration for the Conveyance, or as a result of the provisions of the Companies Act, are hereby extinguished.

*Rights of The Queen and others preserved.*

**5.** Nothing in this Act shall prejudice or affect the rights —

(a) of Her Majesty The Queen, Her Heirs and Successors; or

(b) of any bodies, politic and corporate or any other persons except such as are mentioned in this Act and those claiming by, from and under them.
