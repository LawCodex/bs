# Henryk Podlewski (Private Practice) Act

*[IV – 189*

### HENRYK PODLEWSKI (PRIVATE PRACTICE)

HENRYK PODLEWSKI (PRIVATE PRACTICE) Act No. 66 of 1957

*66 of 1957*

HENRYK PODLEWSKI (PRIVATE PRACTICE) An Act to enable Henryk Podlewski to Engage in Consulting and Private Practice as a Psychiatrist.

[Commencement 15th July, 1957]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Henryk Podlewski (Private Practice) Act.

*Interpretation.*

**2.** In this Act —

“Psychiatrist” means a specialist in that branch of medicine which deals with mental disorders.

*Issue of certificate.*

**3.** (1) Notwithstanding the provisions of the Medical Department Act, or any other Act, it shall be lawful for the Governor in Council to issue a certificate authorizing Henryk Podlewski to engage in consulting and private practice as a Psychiatrist subject to such terms and conditions as may be specified in the certificate.

(2) Such certificate issued under the authority of sub section (1) of this section may at any time be revoked, amended or altered by the Governor in Council.
