# Randol Fawkes Labour Day Act

*[IV – 403*

*9 of 2013.*

MODIFICATION OF PROVISIONS (MAYAGUANA ISLAND DEVELOPERS LIMITED) Act No. 9 of 2013 RANDOL FAWKES LABOUR DAY An Act to rename the Public Holiday known as

“Labour Day” as “Randol Fawkes Labour Day”

[Assent - 16th May, 2013]

[Commencement - 17th May, 2013]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Randol Fawkes Labour Day Act.

*Renaming of Labour Day. Ch. 36.*

**2.** The 1st Friday of June of each year, which is observed as “Labour Day” in accordance with section 3 of the Public Holidays Act, shall hereinafter be observed as

“Randol Fawkes Labour Day”.

*THE BAHAMAS*
