### APPROPRIATION (CAPITAL DEVELOPMENT) (2010/2011) ACT, 2010

AN ACT FOR THE APPROPRIATION OF THE SUM OF TWO HUNDRED AND SIXTY-FIVE MILLION, THREE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-NINE THOUSAND, ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-SEVEN DOLLARS FOR AND TOWARDS DEFRAYING THE EXPENSES OF THE GOVERNMENT OF THE COMMONWEALTH OF THE BAHAMAS FOR CAPITAL DEVELOPMENT FOR THE FINANCIAL YEAR ENDING 30TH JUNE, 2011

(Date of Assent -29th June, 2010)

Enacted by the Parliament of The Bahamas

**1.** Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Appropriation (Capital Development) (20 I 012011) Act, 2010.

**2.** Interpretation.

In this Act, "development expenditure" means expenditure for capital development within The Bahamas.

Authority for payment 3.

(I) There may be paid out of the Treasury upon the authority of a warrant under the hand of the Minister of Finance during the financial year beginning on the I" day of July, 2010 and ending on the 30'h day of June, 2011, the several sums of money in respect of development expenditure set forth in the Schedule.

(2) In the financial year referred to in subsection (I), not more than two hundred and sixty-five million, three hundred and twenty-nine thousand, one hundred and twenty-seven dollars may be paid in total under the provisions of this section.
