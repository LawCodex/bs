# Judges Remuneration and Pensions (Special Provisions) Act

*IV – 386]*

### JUDGES’ REMUNERATION AND PENSIONS (SPECIAL PROVISIONS)

Act No. 11 of 2007

### JUDGES’ REMUNERATION AND PENSIONS (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT

An Act to provide for the payment of pension to Mr. Justice Jeffrey Thompson of the Supreme Court

[Assent 20th March, 2007]

*S.I. 28/2007.*

[Commencement 2nd April, 2007]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may be cited as the Judges’ Remuneration and Pensions (Special Provisions) Act.

*Award of pension.*

*Ch. 45.*

**2.** Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in section 6(1)(b) of the Judges’ Remuneration and Pensions Act, Mr. Justice Jeffrey Thompson, a retired justice, shall be paid a pension or gratuity on the same formula as under the Judges’ Remuneration and Pensions Act.
