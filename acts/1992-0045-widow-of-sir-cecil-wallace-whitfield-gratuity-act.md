# Widow of Sir Cecil Wallace-Whitfield (Gratuity) Act

*IV – 366]*

### WIDOW OF SIR CECIL WALLACE-WHITFIELD (GRATUITY)

### WIDOW OF SIR CECIL WALLACE-WHITFIELD (GRATUITY)

Act No. 45 of 1992

### WIDOW OF SIR CECIL WALLACE-WHITFIELD (GRATUITY)

## ARRANGEMENT OF SECTIONS

**1.** Short title.

**2.** Charge upon the Consolidated Fund.

*45 of 1992*

An Act to make provision out of the Consolidated Fund for a gratuity to be paid to the widow of Sir Cecil Wallace-Whitfield.

[Assent 29th December, 1992]

[Commencement 30th December, 1992]

*Short title.*

**1.** This Act may cited as the Widow of Sir Cecil Wallace-Whitfield (Gratuity) Act, 1992.

*Charge upon the Consolidated Fund.*

**2.** There is hereby charged upon the Consolidated Fund a gratuity in the amount of Fifty Thousand Dollars to be paid to the widow of the late Sir Cecil Wallace-Witfield and which said amount represents the annual salary being paid to the late Sir Cecil Wallace-Witfield at the time of his demise.
