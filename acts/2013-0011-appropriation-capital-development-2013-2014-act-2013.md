Enacted by the Parliament of The Bahamas

**1.** Short title . •

This Act may be cited as the Appropriation (Capital Development) (2013/2014) Act, 2013.

**2.** Interpretation.

In this Act, "development expenditure" means expenditure for capital development within The Bahamas.
