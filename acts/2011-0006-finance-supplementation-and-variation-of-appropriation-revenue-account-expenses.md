# Finance (Supplementation and Variation of Appropriation)(Revenue Account Expenses)(2010) Act, 2011

No. 6 of2011

FINANCE (SUPPLEMENATION AND VARIATION OF APPROPRIATION) (REVENUE ACCOUNT EXPENSES) (2010) ACT, 2011

AN ACT TO SUPPLEMENT AND VARY THE APPROPRIATION OF THE SUM THE ISSUE OF WHICH WAS AUTHORIZED BY THE APPROPRIATION (REVENUE ACCOUNT EXPENSES) (2010/2011) ACT, 2010

[Date of Assent - 1st April, 2011]

Enacted by the Parliament of The Bahamas

**1.** Short title.

This Act may be cited as the Finance (Supplementation and Variation of Appropriation) (Revenue Account Expenses) (20 I 0) Act, 2011.

**2.** Construction of Act No. 9 of 2010.

In this Act and in any other written law, a reference to the Appropriation (Revenue Account Expenses) (2010/2011) Act, 2010 ("the Appropriation Act, 20 1 0") shall be construed as a reference to that Act as for the time being varied or otherwise amended by this Act.

**3.** Increase in Head of Expenditure.

The sums appropriated to the Head of Expenditure set out in the First Column of

Part I of the Schedule to this Act by the Appropriation Act, 20 1 0 are increased by the amounts specified in the Second Column of that Part.

**4.** Reduction in Head of Expenditure.

The sums appropriated to the Head of Expenditure set out in the First Column of

Part II of the Schedule to this Act by the Appropriation Act, 20 l 0 are reduced by the amounts specified in the Second Column of that Part.
